## 1. Why We're Building an AI Personal Agent

The most capable AI in history is already in everyone's pocket. It can plan, research, remember, write, and act. And yet, for almost everyone, it just sits there **waiting to be told what to do**. Power arrived before relationship. People have a genius on call and use it like a search box—not because they are wrong, but because the tools were built for answers, not for **presence**.

We think that is backwards. A capable **AI personal agent** should pay attention, remember what matters, notice change, and show up before you ask. Not a smarter chatbot—a companion that closes the gap between what AI *can* do and what your life actually gets from it.

Three commitments anchor Today: **it knows you**, **it acts before you ask**, and **it walks with you** toward the life you want.

We are not trying to win a benchmark on a single prompt. We are trying to ship the kind of software you **leave running**—because it compounds context the way a great chief of staff compounds, quietly, over quarters. That ambition is why we call Today an **AI personal agent** in public, and why we wrote a separate definition post instead of hiding behind a vague "assistant" label.

## 2. It Knows You — Memory You Can Trust

An agent that does not know you can only answer you; one that knows you can **help**. Most AI meets you as a stranger every time—the tab closes and the context vanishes. That is not an assistant or an agent. That is a vending machine.

![A hand lifting one card from a fan of handwritten index cards on a warm desk](/blog/images/meet-today/meet-today-knows-you.jpg)

**Being known only works if you stay in control.** Today holds memory you can read, edit, and erase—transparency as a design requirement, not a footnote. How that works in the product is documented in [What is Today?](/blog/what-is-today#3-living-memory-your-agent-remembers-your-life); why **personal agent** beats **assistant** as a label is in [What is an AI personal agent?](/blog/what-is-ai-personal-agent).

In early Beta, people tell us the product "gets them." We treat that as the bar: not clever replies, but **continuity** that compounds.

Trust is also **revocable**. Every connector you add should be removable without losing the core agent—conversation, memory, briefs. That design choice reflects how personal agents must behave: present by default, invasive never.

We are not building the loudest assistant in the feed. We are building the one you **keep**—because it remembers, prepares, and waits for your yes.

## 3. It Acts Before You Ask — Agency Without Spam

Knowing you is half the job. What you feel daily is what the agent **does** with that knowledge—quietly, before you ask. A good friend does not wait for a formal request before saying "don't forget tomorrow's thing." That is the proactivity we mean: timed to your day, not broadcast because the model can.

![A phone on a kitchen counter showing a single notification](/blog/images/meet-today/meet-today-proactive.jpg)

Chat is a terrible place to keep a life—everything scrolls away. A personal agent should **surface** what matters in views you return to, not bury it in threads you dig for. And it should work **while you live**: long-running tasks that stay alive across days instead of dying when a chat session ends.

We hold a strict notification bar—details in the product post—because an agent that pings everything gets muted, and then it helps no one.

**Proactivity is not autonomy.** Today will not send email, move calendar events, or message others without your explicit confirm. That line protects trust and keeps the product on the right side of "personal agent" versus "autonomous bot." If you want full autopilot, Today is the wrong tool today—and probably the wrong mental model for software this close to your identity.

## 4. It Walks With You — Life Arcs, Not Errands Only

The point was never to clear today's tasks slightly faster. It is to move your life forward across **months**—fitness, learning, savings, relationships, craft—not one errand at a time. Productivity software optimized the working slice of you. We disagree.

![A walker on a long ridge path through morning haze](/blog/images/meet-today/meet-today-long-thread.jpg)

Early Beta users use Today for work **and** for health routines, language practice, and long projects that do not fit a single prompt. That is the agent we want: one thread held across seasons. Lifestyle health workflows—meal planning, sleep rhythm, training—live on our [Healthcare hub](/healthcare) with clear **lifestyle support, not medical diagnosis** boundaries.

Getting in shape. Learning photography. Preparing for a baby. These are arcs, not checkboxes. An AI personal agent should remember where you are going and nudge you toward it—not reset every Monday.

Consider a founder preparing for a launch while traveling: the agent should connect **flight days**, **sleep debt**, and **promised deliverables** without a daily re-brief. Consider a parent returning to fitness after injury: memory should hold **clearance timing** and **prior load**, suggesting a lighter week before you overreach.

## 5. Capability Without the Hype

We do not lead with the machine, but warmth is not a substitute for follow-through. Under the surface, Today is a real agent stack: model orchestration, planning, memory retrieval, task queues, cross-device sync. We invest in internal evaluations so improvement is measurable—not merely claimed.

The unglamorous work matters: infrastructure that stays responsive, jobs that finish instead of stall, sync that lands on the right screen. **Knows you** and **acts before you ask** are demos until reliability makes them habits.

We also believe **evaluation beats adjectives**. Internally, we test planning, memory retrieval, and connector success rates on scenarios drawn from real Beta days—not synthetic trivia questions. That discipline is how an AI personal agent earns the word **agent** instead of borrowing it for a landing page.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why call it an AI personal agent instead of an assistant?

**Assistant** suggests answers on demand. **Agent** suggests persistence, initiative, and multi-step execution with your approval. Today does all three. We wrote [What is an AI personal agent?](/blog/what-is-ai-personal-agent) so the label means something precise—not marketing fog.

### Why start another AI company now?

Because capability without relationship wastes most of what models can do. The missing layer is not raw intelligence—it is an agent that **stays** with you across days and apps. That is the gap Today targets.

### What does "walks with you" mean in practice?

It means Today holds **long threads**—training blocks, learning goals, savings targets—not only today's calendar. Progress and setbacks feed memory so the next nudge fits where you actually are.

### Can I see and delete what Today remembers?

Yes. Transparency is core to trusting a personal agent. See [What is Today?](/blog/what-is-today) for how memory editing works.

### Is Today only for work?

No. Work is one slice. Health, learning, relationships, and personal projects are first-class—see [Healthcare](/healthcare) for lifestyle examples.

### How do I get access?

Join the [waitlist](/waitlist). Invites roll out as Beta capacity allows. Downloads are on the [downloads page](/downloads) once you have access.

### Is Today finished?

No. We are in early-access Beta, shipping in waves and learning in public.

### Why "personal" matters in AI personal agent

**Personal** is not a marketing softener—it is a scope claim. Enterprise agents optimize tickets; coding agents optimize repos. A personal agent optimizes **your Tuesday** and the six-month arc behind it. That scope is why memory must be inspectable and proactivity must be restrained: you live with the consequences, not an IT admin.

### What would success look like?

Success is not "users open Today more." Success is users **thinking about Today less**—because the trip, the training block, or the contract moved forward without another evening of admin. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.
