## Conclusion 多样结尾示例（避免跨篇模板化）

**预测型**：
> The longer this creator-format economy runs, the more "editing style" becomes a repeatable system rather than a talent lottery. Creators who can name the exact cut pattern in a successful video will spend less time guessing — and more time shipping.

**未解问题型**：
> Whether AI can fully capture a creator's *taste* — the judgment calls on which shot survives the cut — is still open. What's clear is that the mechanical 80% (pacing, order, captions) is already automatable today.

**具体警告型**：
> Don't confuse copying an editing style with copying a person's content. The pacing and caption rhythm are fair game; another creator's original footage, voice, and personality are not. Edit like them, but film like you.

**行动号召型（Workflow 适用）**：
> The fastest way to test this workflow isn't more research — it's one real video. Pick a 10-minute source clip, run it through long-to-short, and compare the first pass against what you'd have cut by hand.
