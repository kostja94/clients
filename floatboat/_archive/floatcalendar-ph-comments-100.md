## E. 对比/品类定位型（8 条）

> 与现有工具对比，突出 FloatBoat 的品类差异。注意：不贬低竞品，强调品类不同。

61. **EN · 中等 · vs Calendly**
    > Calendly solved "when to meet." Motion solved "how to arrange blocks." FloatBoat solves "what to do before, during, and after the meeting." It's not competing with scheduling tools — it's creating the proactive agent category. Important distinction, and the product delivers on it.

62. **EN · 中等 · vs Chat AI**
    > I've tried using ChatGPT for meeting prep. The workflow: copy event details → paste into chat → write a prompt → review output → copy back. Each. Time. FloatBoat does this automatically, per event, without prompts. The difference between "AI tool" and "AI agent" is whether you have to ask. This doesn't wait to be asked.

63. **EN · 短中 · vs 传统 Calendar**
    > Every calendar app tells you WHERE and WHEN. Some tell you WHO. None of them tell you WHAT TO SAY and WHAT TO FOLLOW UP ON. FloatBoat fills that gap. It's not a better calendar — it's a layer on top of your existing calendar that makes it actionable.

64. **EN · 中等 · vs Motion/Reclaim**
    > Motion and Reclaim are optimizing for *time allocation*. FloatBoat is optimizing for *work execution*. Different problems. I use Motion to schedule, FloatBoat to prepare and follow up. They're complementary, not competitive. The combo is actually great — schedule with Motion, execute with FloatBoat.

65. **EN · 中等 · vs 虚拟 EA 服务**
    > I priced out virtual EA services for meeting prep — $1,500-3,000/month for part-time support. FloatBoat's Pro plan is a fraction of that and handles 80% of what I'd delegate. For a bootstrap startup, this is the obvious first hire. EA for the rest of us.

66. **EN · 短中 · vs Zapier 自动化**
    > I built a Zapier + ChatGPT + Notion pipeline to do roughly what FloatBoat does in one native app. It took 6 hours to set up, broke twice, and cost $50/month in API calls. FloatBoat took 4 minutes to set up. Sometimes the integrated solution really is better than the DIY one.

67. **EN · 中等 · vs Slack AI**
    > Our team tried using Slack AI summaries for meeting context. The problem: it's reactive — summarizes after the fact, doesn't prep beforehand. FloatBoat's "proactive" positioning is the key differentiator. Prep before, summarize after. That's the full loop most tools miss.

68. **EN · 短中 · vs Notion Calendar**
    > Notion Calendar is great for visibility. FloatBoat is great for actionability. One shows me what's coming. The other prepares me for it. I use both, but FloatBoat has become the one I actually *rely* on before walking into a call.

    > From an architecture standpoint, the design decision to anchor on calendar events as triggers — rather than building another chat interface — is the right call.     > The local-first desktop architecture is the right bet for calendar data. Uploading your entire schedule to a cloud AI is a privacy nightmare waiting to happen. 79. **EN · 短中 · MCP/A2A 兼容**
    > Does FloatBoat speak MCP? The Combo Skills concept maps naturally to MCP tools — calendar events as tool triggers. 80. **EN · 中等 · Mac 技术实现**
    > Curious about the Mac implementation details. Is the floating window using Accessibility API or a custom overlay? The fact that it doesn't steal focus while staying on top is non-trivial on macOS. 81. **EN · 中等 · Agent 自主性层级**
