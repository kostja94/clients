## 3. Snippet-ready 定义句（范例）

> An agentic calendar is a calendar-based AI system that proactively executes work triggered by scheduled events — gathering context before meetings, producing deliverables against deadlines, and automating follow-up after — without requiring manual prompts for each action.

## 4. Conclusion 多样结尾示例

每篇 conclusion 用不同的收束方式（避免跨篇模板化）：

- **预测**: "As Calendar-Driven AI matures, the line between 'scheduling tool' and 'operating system' will blur further — agents won't just manage time, they'll execute the work that fills it."
- **未解问题**: "Whether the Calendar-Driven paradigm translates equally well to async-first distributed teams — where 'events' are less structured than 'deadlines' — remains an open question for the next generation of agent design."
- **具体警告**: "Don't confuse agentic calendars with smart schedulers. The difference isn't degrees of automation — it's who initiates the work. A scheduler waits for you to tell it when; an agentic calendar knows when and acts."
- **反向视角**: "The real test of an agentic calendar isn't what it can automate — it's what happens when it gets something wrong. Recovery patterns, not feature lists, will separate the category leaders."
