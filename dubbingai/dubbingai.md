## 1. Product Overview

**One-line description**:
```
Dubbing AI is a real-time AI voice changer for gamers, streamers, and content creators—with 500+ character voices (games, anime, celebrities), 100,000+ meme sound effects, <30ms latency, and no pre-recording required—so you sound like anyone, anywhere, instantly.
```

**Category**: B2C / AI Voice / Real-Time Voice Changer / Soundboard / Gaming & Streaming  
**Business model**: 免费下载 + 高级订阅（具体待官网确认）  
**Pricing**: 免费基础版；高级/订阅更多声音、音效、Voice Cloning；Dubbing Box 硬件（shop.dubbingai.io）

**产品形态**：
- **桌面 App**：dubbingai.io 下载（与 Voicemod、Voice.ai 等竞品同为 Desktop，因低延迟需本地处理）
- **在线版**：Online Voice Changer
- **硬件 Dubbing Box**：[shop.dubbingai.io](https://shop.dubbingai.io/)——**竞品均为 Desktop，Dubbing AI 独有硬件**，支持 Android、Switch、Xbox、PS5、PC，sub-20ms 延迟，内置 AI 芯片 + NPU
- **中文站**：dubbing.tech

**核心功能**（双核心）：
- **Real-time Voice Changer**：实时 AI 变声，500+ 角色、<30ms 延迟、超低 CPU（<3%）
- **Soundboard**：100,000+ meme 音效，直播/游戏即插即用

**其他产品线**：Voice Cloning、Online Voice Changer、**Dubbing Box**（硬件差异化——竞品无硬件，仅 Desktop）

**数据规模**：500+ 角色声音、100,000+ meme 音效、<30ms 延迟、<3% CPU、40+ 语言  
**代表用户**：jollztv (606K)、tinymacdude (796K)、Lifelessanton (755K) 等 YouTuber/Twitch 主播

## 2. Positioning Statement

> **For** gamers, streamers, content creators, and meeting users **who** want to sound like game characters, anime voices, or celebrities in real-time without pre-recording, **our** Dubbing AI **is a** real-time AI voice changer **that** offers 500+ character voices, 100,000+ meme sound effects, <30ms latency, and works with Discord, Zoom, VRchat, OBS, and games. **Unlike** traditional voice changers with limited voices and high latency, **we** deliver instant character transformation with minimal CPU usage **because** we're built for real-time gaming, streaming, and social—no setup, no delay.

## 4. Target Audience / ICP

**Primary ICP**:
- **Who**：游戏玩家、主播、内容创作者、会议用户
- **Industry**：游戏、直播、短视频、社交、远程会议
- **Jobs to be done**：游戏内变声、直播变声、短视频配音、会议/通话变声、社交娱乐
- **Pain points**：传统变声器延迟高、声音少、占用高；需要预录；平台兼容性差
- **Buying triggers**：需要实时变声、需要角色/动漫声音、需要 meme 音效、需要低延迟

**Secondary ICP**：VRChat 用户、Roblox 创作者、短视频创作者

- **Gaps to exploit**: Voicemod alternative、Voice.ai alternative、real-time voice changer、voice changer for gaming、meme soundboard
