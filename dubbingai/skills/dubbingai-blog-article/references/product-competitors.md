### 6.3 Voicemod battlecard（P6 公平）

**When Voicemod wins**
- Creator already standardized on Voicemod + Stream Deck workflow
- Wants VoiceLab deep customization on Pro
- Prefers longest-running streamer brand familiarity

**When Dubbing AI wins**
- Soundboard + voice in one stack with very large library narrative
- Game/anime preset depth (Gojo, Valorant, etc.)
- Dubbing Box for console/mobile hardware path
- Lower CPU narrative for game + OBS simultaneously

**Pricing (verify)**：Free + Pro ~$50/yr — cite [Voicemod official support/pricing](https://support.voicemod.net/) as of check date.

### 6.4 Voice.ai battlecard

**When Voice.ai wins**
- Maximum community voice catalog exploration
- Cloning-forward persona workflows
- SDK/embed use cases

**When Dubbing AI wins**
- Bundled meme soundboard scale for streams
- Curated game/anime landing pages + hardware option
