## What the Shop Performance Score is

The SPS is a dynamic rating from 0 to 5 that measures how well your shop meets [TikTok Shop seller standards](https://seller.tiktok.com/) across product quality, delivery, and customer experience. It is refreshed on a rolling window — the inputs are evaluated over roughly the last 60 days — so your score reflects recent behavior, not a permanent grade. A strong score compounds into more visibility and faster payouts; a weak one quietly throttles your distribution before you see the number move.

You do not have an SPS until your shop has delivered at least 30 eligible orders within the last 90 days — orders that are fake, sample, or gift are excluded. Below that threshold, your score is null rather than low, and the distinction matters because TikTok treats a null SPS differently from a low SPS in several eligibility checks.

The score is weighted by your primary category. TikTok evaluates your metrics against benchmarks from your best-selling category over the last 90 days, not against a universal standard. A return rate that is normal in one category can look terrible in another, which is why comparing your score to sellers in different niches is misleading.

## The three pillars and six metrics

The SPS is built from three pillars, each carrying a set of metrics. Understanding the exact inputs is the difference between guessing and acting.

| Pillar | Metric | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Product satisfaction | Negative Review Rate | Share of delivered orders with 1–2 star reviews |
| Product satisfaction | Non-Buyer-Fault Return/Refund Rate | Returns caused by you or your logistics partner |
| Fulfillment & logistics | Seller-Fault Cancellation Rate | Orders you cancel for stockouts, pricing, or shipping errors |
| Fulfillment & logistics | On-Time Delivery Rate | Share of orders delivered by their deliver-by date |
| Customer service | IM Dissatisfaction Rate | Share of chats rated 1–2 stars by buyers |
| Customer service | After-Sales Handling Time | How long you take to process refunds and returns |

Product satisfaction measures whether buyers are happy with what they receive — negative reviews, and returns that are your fault or your logistics partner's fault. Fulfillment measures whether you ship on time and avoid cancelling orders for your own reasons. Customer service measures how responsive you are in chat and how fast you resolve after-sales requests.

The weighting of these six metrics shifts by category, but the shape of a healthy shop is consistent across all of them: accurate listings, on-time fulfillment, and fast, human service. If any one pillar collapses, your score follows — and because the score is a lagging indicator on a 60-day window, a bad two weeks now will show up in your number later, not today.

## What your score unlocks

The SPS is not just a report card — it is a gate. Different score bands unlock different platform capabilities, and TikTok's official guidance sets specific thresholds for each.

| Score band | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| 2.5+ | Flash Deal eligibility |
| 3.5+ | Affiliate marketing access, campaign participation |
| 4.0+ | CRM permissions, faster settlement, Star Seller badge eligibility |
| 4.5+ | Broader campaign and premium traffic access |

The 3.5 threshold is the one most sellers care about, because it controls affiliate marketing. At 3.5 you can create affiliate plans and collaborate with creators on commission-based promotion. Drop below 3.0 and active affiliate marketing is terminated — existing affiliate plans and campaign products are removed — and you must climb back to 3.5 to regain eligibility. TikTok documents these thresholds in Seller Center's performance score guidance; check your live dashboard for current band definitions because TikTok updates eligibility copy periodically.

Settlement speed is the less obvious lever. Higher score bands unlock accelerated settlement cycles, compressing payout times from as long as eight days down to as little as one. For a shop running on thin margins, that difference in cash-flow timing is worth real money — and it is a direct reason to treat the SPS as a financial metric, not a vanity score.

When a metric spikes, treat it as a SKU-level problem first. A single high-return product can drag the entire product-satisfaction pillar; pausing that SKU often moves the score faster than shop-wide policy changes. The action table below maps each metric to a first response — use it as a weekly ops checklist, not a one-time cleanup.

## What happens when your score drops

A low SPS does not trigger a fixed penalty the way a policy violation does. Instead, it gates features and distribution, which means the damage compounds over time. Sellers in the lowest tiers face settlement cycles as long as 31 days and reserve rates up to 70 percent — meaning most of your revenue is frozen while you still pay for inventory, ads, and shipping.

Below 3.5, you cannot recruit affiliate creators or join platform campaigns, which removes two of the main growth channels for a TikTok Shop. Below 3.0, active affiliate marketing is terminated entirely. And across every band, lower scores mean less organic visibility, because the algorithm routes distribution toward reliable sellers.

The compounding effect is what makes the SPS dangerous to ignore. A shop that loses affiliate access cannot create new content momentum, which keeps sales flat, which leaves the score low — a spiral that is much harder to escape than to avoid. ## How to improve your SPS

Improving the SPS is a matter of moving the six metrics, and each metric has a specific set of actions attached to it. This is the action map that turns the score from an abstract number into a to-do list.

| Metric | Action to improve it |
|---|---|
| Negative Review Rate | Fix listing accuracy; order samples; realistic mockups |
| Return/Refund Rate | Pause SKUs with return rates above 8%; tighten listings |
| Seller-Fault Cancellation | Keep stock accurate; stop listing out-of-stock items |
| On-Time Delivery | Use reliable carriers; ship within the lead time you set |
| IM Dissatisfaction | Respond within 2 hours; use auto-replies for coverage |
| After-Sales Handling | Resolve refund/return requests within 48 hours |

Product satisfaction improves through listing accuracy — the leading cause of complaints is a listing that misleads. Test products before selling, use realistic mockups that match the final product, and monitor return rates by SKU. If a specific product's return rate climbs above 8 percent, pause it; one bad SKU drags the whole pillar down.

Fulfillment improves through predictability more than speed. A shop that ships in 48 hours every time scores better than one that ships in 24 hours sometimes, because the on-time delivery rate rewards consistency. Set your lead time in Seller Center to match your real production time, use reliable carriers with valid tracking, and keep stock levels accurate to avoid cancellations.

Customer service improves through response discipline. TikTok's own data shows that delaying a chat response by a single hour spikes your 1–2 star chat rating by roughly 5 percent. Respond within two hours where possible, use auto-replies to cover gaps, and resolve refund and return requests within 48 hours — the recommended after-sales target. ## Common score-damaging mistakes

Most SPS damage comes from a handful of avoidable mistakes, and they all share a theme: treating operations as an afterthought until the score drops.

The first mistake is choosing the wrong product category. Your metrics are benchmarked against your category, so a product placed in a category with lower return rates makes your numbers look worse than they are. Listing accurately — both the product and the category — is a free way to protect your score.

The second mistake is ignoring late dispatches. Keeping the late dispatch rate under 4 percent and the seller-fault cancellation rate under 2.5 percent protects both your SPS and your Account Health Rating, because crossing those thresholds can trigger point deductions and extended settlement periods in parallel.

The third mistake is letting refunds sit unanswered. Refund and return requests that linger are scored against your after-sales handling time, and they compound into negative reviews when buyers get frustrated. Fixing a return quickly costs less than absorbing the double penalty of a slow resolution and a bad rating.

The fourth mistake is treating the score as a one-time fix. The SPS is a rolling 60-day indicator, so a single good week does not move it, and a single bad week does not sink it — but sustained drift in either direction shows up in the trailing window. The sellers who keep high scores are the ones who run operations against these metrics every week, not just when the number drops.

## SPS vs Account Health Rating

The SPS is often confused with a second seller metric — the Account Health Rating (AHR) — and they measure different things.

| Metric | Scale | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Shop Performance Score (SPS) | 0–5 | Results: product, fulfillment, service quality |
| Account Health Rating (AHR) | 0–1,000 | Policy compliance and violations |

The AHR is the enforcement system. New sellers start with 200 points, lose points for policy violations, and gain points for completed orders and passing policy quizzes. Milestone enforcement kicks in at 150, 100, 50, and 0 points, with consequences ranging from deferred settlement to fund suspension.

The SPS is the results system. It does not track violations — it tracks how well you deliver, fulfill, and support. The two interact: operations that damage your SPS (late dispatches, cancellations) can also trigger AHR point deductions, which is why the thresholds like 4 percent late dispatch and 2.5 percent cancellation matter twice.

For a healthy shop, watch both numbers. The SPS tells you how your buyers experience your shop; the AHR tells you how TikTok views your compliance. A shop can have a perfect SPS and still lose the account on AHR — or never reach high SPS because enforcement actions are suppressing it. Secure the TikTok login tied to Seller Center with [two-step verification](/blog/tiktok-two-step-verification) as part of the same hygiene routine — account takeover stops operations regardless of score.

The TikTok Shop Performance Score is the gatekeeper of your shop's growth: it controls visibility, affiliate access, campaign eligibility, and settlement speed, and it compounds in both directions. Improving it is not mysterious. Fix listing accuracy and pause high-return SKUs to protect product satisfaction. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good TikTok Shop Performance Score?

A score of 3.5 or higher is the practical target for most sellers, because it unlocks affiliate marketing access and campaign participation. Scores of 4.0 and above unlock CRM permissions, faster settlement, and Star Seller eligibility. The score is benchmarked against your category, so "good" is relative to how your competitors in the same niche perform.

### How is the SPS score calculated?

The SPS is a 0–5 rating built from six metrics across three pillars: product satisfaction (negative review rate, non-buyer-fault return rate), fulfillment and logistics (seller-fault cancellation rate, on-time delivery rate), and customer service (IM dissatisfaction rate, after-sales handling time). It runs on a rolling 60-day window, refreshes regularly, and weights metrics relative to your primary category.

### When do I get a Shop Performance Score?

You receive an SPS once your shop has delivered at least 30 eligible orders within the last 90 days — excluding fake, sample, and gift orders. Below that threshold your score is null, which is treated differently from a low score in eligibility calculations.

### How do I improve my TikTok Shop performance score?

Focus on the six metrics: fix listing accuracy and pause SKUs with return rates above 8 percent, keep the late dispatch rate under 4 percent and seller-fault cancellation under 2.5 percent, respond to customer messages within two hours, and resolve refund and return requests within 48 hours. The score reflects a rolling window, so sustained improvement — not one good week — is what moves it.

### What happens if my SPS is below 3.0?

Below 3.0, active affiliate marketing and campaigns are terminated — existing affiliate plans and campaign products are removed. Lower scores also mean reduced visibility, longer settlement cycles (up to 31 days in the lowest tiers), and higher reserve rates. You must reach 3.5 again to regain affiliate access.
