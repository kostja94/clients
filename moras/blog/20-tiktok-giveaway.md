## TL;DR

Giveaways are one of the fastest ways to grow a TikTok following — a single well-run giveaway can add thousands of followers in a week. TikTok giveaways can spike followers fast — but after **February 25, 2026** rule changes, official terms must appear **on the video itself**, not only in the caption, and pay-to-enter mechanics (Coins, gifts) cross into gambling territory. FTC disclosure still requires clear sponsorship language. Shop giveaways differ from organic posts and from LIVE auction formats — each has separate compliance surfaces.

- Put **complete official rules on-video** — eligibility, prize, deadline, selection method, sponsor
- No **Coins or paid gifts** as entry fees; no wheel-spin random selection on Shop listings
- Use TikTok's **LIVE Giveaway** tool when running giveaways inside a Shop LIVE session
- Match prize to niche — generic high-value prizes attract followers who unfollow after the draw

## Why giveaways work on TikTok

A giveaway works on TikTok for a simple reason: it converts passive viewers into active participants. A video that asks people to comment or join a live stream generates engagement signals the algorithm rewards, which pushes the content to more viewers, which brings more participants — a compounding loop that a normal post cannot produce.

The mechanics matter more than the prize. Giveaways that grow accounts do not ask for a single generic action; they give people a reason to engage repeatedly and a reason to bring friends. That is why the most effective giveaways pair a desirable prize with a simple, repeatable entry action.

But the growth loop only runs while the post stays up. A giveaway that violates TikTok's rules gets removed, the engagement vanishes, and repeat violations can restrict the account. The discipline of compliance is not a formality — it is what protects the growth. Every rule in the rest of this article exists because breaking it costs more than the giveaway was worth.

## The two rulebooks: Shop prizes vs organic

The single most important decision in a TikTok giveaway is which rulebook you are under, because it determines almost everything else. The dividing line is the prize: if the prize comes from TikTok Shop, you must use TikTok's official LIVE Giveaway feature and follow its narrow rules. If it does not, you have more design freedom but still must disclose the terms.

| Aspect | Shop giveaway | Organic giveaway |
|---|---|---|
| Tool | Official LIVE Giveaway feature | Any method you choose |
| Prize cap | Under $500 | No TikTok cap (state laws apply) |
| Entry methods | Join to win or comment to win only | Any free entry method |
| Extra actions | None allowed (no follow/tag/share requirements) | Design freedom |
| Cash / gift cards | Prohibited | State and FTC rules apply |
| Non-affiliation line | Required | Required |

For a Shop giveaway, TikTok is strict: you must use the LIVE Giveaway feature, the prize must be an actual product valued under $500, cash and gift cards are prohibited, and the only entry methods are join-to-win or comment-to-win. You cannot require follows, tags, shares, or purchases. The rules are narrow, but they are clear.

For an organic giveaway, you have more freedom to design entry methods — follow, comment, duet, or hashtag entries all work. The constraints are the general legal ones: entry must be free, terms must be disclosed, and high-value prizes may trigger state registration requirements. The trade-off is that organic giveaways are less visible to TikTok's own Shop distribution.

## The 2026 disclosure rules you must follow

The February 2026 policy update made one change that matters more than any other: giveaway terms must now be visible on the video itself, not just in the caption or a link.

| Required term | Where it must appear |
|---|---|
| Prize description and rough value | On-video (text overlay or spoken) |
| Eligibility (age, location) | On-video |
| Exact entry steps | On-video |
| Winner selection method | On-video |
| End date | On-video |
| Non-affiliation line | In content |

The practical rule: if a viewer cannot see or hear the key terms while watching the video, TikTok treats the post as non-compliant. A caption alone no longer counts. Put the prize and its value, who can enter, how to enter, how the winner is chosen, and when it ends on screen as text overlays, or state them aloud — and for live streams, repeat them regularly.

The non-affiliation line matters across both rulebooks: state that your promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with TikTok. It is a short sentence that protects the account from being flagged.

Finally, entry must be free — no purchase necessary. This is a US legal requirement for sweepstakes under [FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/endorsements-influencers-and-creators), not just a TikTok preference, and it applies to every giveaway regardless of which rulebook you are under.

## Running a compliant Shop giveaway

If the prize is a TikTok Shop product, the compliance path is well defined. TikTok requires the official LIVE Giveaway feature, and the setup looks like this:

1. Confirm the prize is a product valued under $500 — not cash, not a gift card.
2. Open the LIVE Giveaway feature and create the giveaway with the prize, a join-to-win or comment-to-win entry, and a claim deadline.
3. State the terms on screen during the live: prize value, eligibility, how to enter, winner selection, end date, and the non-affiliation line.
4. Do not require any extra action — no follows, tags, shares, or purchases.
5. Ship the prize within the standard fulfillment timeline.

Two Shop-specific traps are worth flagging. First, you cannot ask viewers to send paid gifts or coins as an entry — that is pay-to-enter, which crosses the line into gambling territory. Second, you cannot list the giveaway item as a normal product or run a gamified wheel-spin selection. If a Shop giveaway involves bidding or auction-style mechanics, it moves into different territory entirely — our [TikTok live auction guide](/blog/tiktok-live-auction) covers those binding-bid rules separately.

The LIVE Giveaway feature also connects to the broader live-selling workflow. If you are running giveaways as part of live shopping sessions, our [TikTok Live Manager guide](/blog/tiktok-live-manager) covers the console where the giveaway is created and managed.

## Running an organic giveaway

An organic giveaway — where the prize is not a TikTok Shop product — gives you more room to design the promotion, and that freedom is where the growth tactics live.

The format that works best for follower growth is a comment-to-enter giveaway with a clear, single action: "comment your favorite product for a chance to win." Keep the entry simple, because friction kills participation. A giveaway that requires following three accounts, tagging five friends, and using a specific hashtag loses most of its entrants at each additional step.

The structure of a strong organic giveaway:

1. Choose a prize your audience actually wants — relevance beats price.
2. Define the single entry action — usually a comment or a follow.
3. Set a clear end date and announce the winner visibly.
4. Display the terms on video: prize, value, eligibility, entry, selection, end date.
5. Include the non-affiliation line.

The prize choice deserves more thought than most creators give it. A prize that matches your niche attracts followers who will stay — a skincare creator giving away skincare products, a pet creator giving away pet gear. A generic high-value prize attracts a crowd that unfollows the moment the giveaway ends. ## FTC disclosure and legal basics

Beyond TikTok's own rules, sponsored giveaways sit under US federal and state law, and the FTC's endorsement guides are the baseline every creator should know.

If a brand sponsors your giveaway or you have a material connection to a product — payment, free products, an affiliate relationship — you must disclose that connection clearly and conspicuously. A "#ad" or "Paid partnership with [Brand]" at the start of your caption, before any truncation, is the minimum. TikTok's Branded Content toggle is mandatory for branded content but is not sufficient on its own — you still need clear disclosure language, and on-screen text or verbal disclosure during the video.

The legal layers to check for a US giveaway:

| Legal item | When it applies |
|---|---|
| No purchase necessary | Always (US sweepstakes law) |
| FTC disclosure | Sponsored or affiliate connection |
| State registration | Large prizes (e.g., NY and FL thresholds) |
| 1099 reporting | Prizes over $600 |
| COPPA | Avoid under-13 entrants |

Entry must always be free, which is the sweepstakes-law baseline. For high-value prizes, check whether your state requires sweepstakes registration — New York and Florida have notable thresholds — and be aware that prizes over $600 may trigger 1099 reporting. Keep eligibility at 18+ to stay clear of COPPA complications.

## Giveaway mistakes that get posts removed

Most giveaway violations are preventable, and they cluster into a handful of patterns. Avoiding them is the difference between a giveaway that grows your account and one that gets it restricted.

The first mistake is burying the terms in the caption. Since February 2026, that alone makes the post non-compliant. The terms must be on the video — every giveaway you run should include on-screen or spoken terms from the start.

The second mistake is requiring purchases or paid actions. Entry must be free, and asking viewers to send gifts or coins to enter crosses the line into pay-to-enter. This is the fastest way to lose a giveaway and draw a policy review.

The third mistake is misleading engagement tactics. TikTok explicitly bans promising a prize for every entry, manufacturing fake urgency, requiring excessive tagging, and making false partnership claims. These tactics do not just violate policy — they signal to TikTok that the engagement is manufactured, which devalues the giveaway in the algorithm.

The fourth mistake is using a non-random winner selection. If you promise a random draw, the selection must actually be random and ideally recordable. A winner picked by "whoever I like best" when you promised random selection is both unfair and a policy violation.

The fifth mistake is ignoring the non-affiliation line. It is one sentence, it applies to every giveaway, and its absence is a common trigger for post removal.

## Conclusion

A TikTok giveaway is a growth engine that runs only while it is compliant. The two-rulebook framework decides your path: a Shop prize means the official LIVE Giveaway feature, a prize under $500, join-to-win or comment-to-win only, and no cash or gift cards — while an organic giveaway gives you design freedom within the requirement that entry stays free and terms are disclosed.

The 2026 rules are clear about presentation: the prize, value, eligibility, entry steps, winner selection, and end date must be visible on the video itself, and the non-affiliation line belongs in every promotion. Add [FTC disclosure](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) when there is a brand connection, and a giveaway that grows your following is also one that survives the platform's review.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I run a TikTok giveaway?

Choose your prize, pick a single entry action, display the terms on the video — prize, value, eligibility, entry steps, winner selection, end date — and keep entry free. If the prize is a TikTok Shop product, use the official LIVE Giveaway feature with a prize under $500. Include the non-affiliation line in every giveaway.

### What are the TikTok giveaway rules in 2026?

The February 25, 2026 policy update requires giveaway terms to be visible on the video itself, not just in the caption. Entry must be free. Shop prizes must use the LIVE Giveaway feature, stay under $500, and cannot be cash or gift cards. Misleading engagement tactics and pay-to-enter are prohibited.

### Can I require follows, tags, or shares to enter a TikTok giveaway?

For TikTok Shop giveaways, no — the only allowed entry methods are join-to-win and comment-to-win, and no extra actions can be required. For organic giveaways, you have more freedom, but requiring excessive tagging is banned as a misleading engagement tactic.

### Do I need to disclose a sponsored TikTok giveaway?

Yes. If a brand sponsors your giveaway or you have a material connection to a product, FTC rules require clear and conspicuous disclosure —"#ad" or "Paid partnership with [Brand]" at the start of the caption, plus on-screen or verbal disclosure. TikTok's Branded Content toggle is mandatory but not sufficient on its own.

### Can TikTok Shop giveaways offer cash or gift cards?

No. TikTok prohibits cash and gift cards as TikTok Shop giveaway prizes. The prize must be an actual product valued under $500. For organic giveaways outside TikTok Shop, cash prizes may be allowed but state sweepstakes laws and tax reporting requirements may apply.
