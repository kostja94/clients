### Snippet-ready 定义句（范例）

> Search 'TikTok hooks' and you will find the same list recycled across 40 blog posts: 'Stop scrolling,' 'You need this,' 'I tried it so you don't have to.' These are not hooks. They are sentence starters. A hook that works is not a phrase you memorize — it is a psychological mechanism matched to a specific product type. This article replaces the list with a framework.
