## Conclusion 多样结尾示例

每篇 conclusion 用不同的收束方式（避免跨篇模板化）：

**预测型**：
> The next few years will decide whether "reaction video" remains a manual craft or becomes an AI-assisted one. The creators who treat the tool as a first-pass editor — not an autopilot — will keep the thing audiences actually watch for: a real person's genuine reaction.

**未解问题型**：
> Whether AI highlight detection can match a creator's own instinct for which moment is "the one" remains an open question. For now, the best workflows put the AI's candidates on the timeline and let the creator make the final cut — the software proposes, the human disposes.

**具体警告型**：
> Don't confuse an auto-generated reaction with an edited one. A generator produces a video without your face. An editor like Vatt organizes footage you actually shot. If your goal is a real, monetisable channel with your own presence, the two paths diverge quickly.

**行动号召型（仅 Product/Scenario 适用）**：
> The 11-step workflow from import to export isn't a marketing diagram — it's the order the tool actually walks you through. The hard part isn't learning the steps. It's trusting the AI's rough cut enough to press play on your own channel.
