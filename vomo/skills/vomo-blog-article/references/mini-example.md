## Conclusion 多样结尾示例

每篇 conclusion 用不同的收束方式（避免跨篇模板化）：

**预测型**：
> As transcription moves from a nice-to-have to table stakes for podcast and meeting content, the tools that let you *keep* your transcript — searchable, exportable, queryable — will pull ahead of platforms that only show it to you. VOMO is built for that second world.

**未解问题型**：
> Whether platforms like Spotify will eventually let users export transcripts natively remains an open question. Until then, the practical answer for anyone who wants to reuse episode content — show notes, SRT subtitles, blog posts — is to transcribe it on your own terms, with tools that let you export.

**具体警告型**：
> Don't confuse "viewing a transcript" with "owning a transcript." Spotify and Apple let you read along. They don't let you search your archive, clip quotes, or export SRT for video. If your workflow needs any of those, a dedicated transcription tool isn't optional — it's the missing step.

**行动号召型（仅 HowTo / WorkflowUseCase 适用）**：
> The whole workflow takes about 15 minutes for a one-hour episode: upload or paste the link, wait for the transcript, and export what you need. The first time you clip a quote straight from an interview, you'll wonder why you ever did this manually.
