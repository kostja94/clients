## 正文（已发）

I shipped my first product.

For a long time, I treated OG images as a branding checkbox — one generic image for every page.

I was wrong.

When a link gets shared on LinkedIn, Slack, or X, that preview image is your ad.

When you're doing programmatic SEO at scale, every URL needs its own visual — not the same logo stretched across 500 pages.

I saw this on client work.

Doing SEO for an AI Notes Generator, we built customized Open Graph images for each page — aligned with the content, ready for social distribution.

Shares looked professional. Click-through improved. It wasn't subtle.

That's when I stopped thinking of OG images as decoration — and started treating them as part of the SEO + distribution stack.

So I built Oginify — my first product.

Paste a URL. AI reads the page. Four social share images (1200×630), content-aware — not one template for everything.

Swipe through the carousel — same URL, four different visuals. That's the product.

I'm building in public. I'll post here at every milestone.

Try it, tell me what's missing, or just follow along — link in the first comment.

If you're scaling pages and still using one OG image for everything — how are you handling it?
