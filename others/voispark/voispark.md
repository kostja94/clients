```
VoiSpark is an AI voice generator platform for content creators that offers text-to-speech, voice cloning, and voice changer—with 1,200+ voices including celebrities across 9 categories, emotion control, and 15-second instant cloning—so you sound like a real human without technical skills.
```

## 2. Positioning Statement

> **For** content creators—YouTubers, podcasters, marketers, educators, and performers—**who** need human-like AI voices for shorts, audiobooks, ads, and narration without sounding robotic, **our** VoiSpark **is an** AI voice generator platform **that** combines 1,200+ voices across 9 categories, 15-second voice cloning, and emotion control in one place—integrating ElevenLabs, Cartesia, MiniMax, OpenAI, and more. **Unlike** single-provider tools that lock you into one model, **we** let you switch between the best AI engines instantly and access 7x more voice options **because** we're built for creators who want expressive, authentic voices that perform like real people.

## 4. Target Audience / ICP

**Primary ICP**:
- **Who**: 内容创作者——短视频创作者（YouTube Shorts、TikTok、Reels）、播客主、营销人、教育者、配音演员、活动策划
- **Industry**: 内容创作、营销、教育、娱乐、活动
- **Jobs to be done**: 为视频/播客/有声书/广告配音；克隆自己的声音；使用名人风格声音；多角色旁白
- **Pain points**: 传统 TTS 机械、单一模型选择有限、克隆需大量样本、无情感控制
- **Buying triggers**: 需要规模化配音、需要名人风格、需要克隆自己声音、需要多语言

**Secondary ICP**: 企业（品牌配音、客服、培训）、游戏/元宇宙（NPC 配音）、无障碍（语音恢复）

**Use Case Personas**（官网分类）：
- The Short-Form Creator（YouTube Shorts、Reels、TikTok、Meme、Parody）
- The Storyteller / Narrator（Podcaster、Audiobook、Course）
- The Marketer（Brand Voice、Ad Voiceover、Campaign Narrator）
- The Event Planner（Event Announcer、MC、Community Broadcaster）
- The Educator（E-learning、Webinar、Training）
- The Performer（Voice Actor、ASMR、Roleplay）

**Language / locale**: 英文为主；支持 30+ 语言（欧洲、亚洲、中东、非洲口音）

- **Direct**: ElevenLabs、PlayHT、Murf AI、Descript、Resemble AI、Lovo

- **Gaps to exploit**: 多模型选择、名人声音库、15 秒克隆、情感标签、性价比
