### 1. For Real Estate Agents | /real-estate

**回答**：房产经纪在什么情境下会用 Collov？

**URL**: /real-estate  
**Title**: AI Virtual Staging for Realtors | Collov AI  
**Meta Description**: Stage empty rooms instantly with AI virtual staging. Sell vacant properties 73% faster. Trusted by 10,000+ agents. Furniture removal, edit, high-res downloads. Try 2 rooms free.

**目标关键词**：AI virtual staging for realtors, virtual staging real estate, real estate agent staging, virtual staging for empty rooms, best virtual staging for real estate agents, virtual staging software for realtors

**搜索意图**：Commercial（选型、比较 best X for realtors）；Transactional（try free、sign up）

**竞品语境**：BoxBrownie 48h、$24/张；Apply Design 10 分钟；StageHQ $0.28/张。Collov 订阅制 $19/月起、15 秒出图、多风格+Twilight+照片编辑。

#### 典型场景（When to Use）

- **空房挂牌**：Vacant listing 需要让买家想象居住空间
- **多房源周转**：同时管理多套 listing，无法逐套实体软装
- **MLS 照片优化**：现有照片杂乱、光线差，需快速增强
- **日转暮**：Twilight 提升 listing 浏览量
- **家具移除**：房主遗留家具需虚拟移除后重新软装

#### Headline（Scenario-first）

When you need to stage vacant listings at scale, Collov helps you go from empty room to MLS-ready in 15 seconds.

#### Problem（场景痛点）

- 空房难卖：Vacant listings sit longer; buyers struggle to envision the space
- 实体软装贵：Physical staging costs $1,500–4,000+ per property
- 照片质量差：Low-quality or cluttered photos hurt listing appeal
- 多房源周转慢：Manual staging can't scale across dozens of listings

#### Solution（工作流）

One-click AI virtual staging. Upload a photo—with or without furniture—choose room type and style (Scandinavian, Modern, Luxury, etc.), get photorealistic results in seconds. Furniture removal, furniture edit, high-res MLS-ready downloads. *能力详情见 [collov-features.md](./collov-features.md)*

#### Proof（简要）

73% faster、78% more buyers、20% higher offers；10,000+ agents。*完整指标与案例见 [Sell Properties Faster](./collov-solutions.md#1-sell-properties-faster--real-estate)*
