**产品形态**：Web App（openmart.com）、app.openmart.com。各 Use Case 页面 CTA 链至 注册、Book demo。

**Use Cases 与 Features、Solutions 严格区分**（基于 skills：use-cases-page-generator、solutions-page-generator、features-page-generator）：

| 类型 | 回答的问题 | 聚焦 | 示例 |
|------|------------|------|------|
| **Features** | 产品**能做什么**？ | Capabilities（能力） | Google Maps Scraper、Owner Finder、Local Business Database |
| **Use Cases** | **谁**在**什么情境**下用？ | Scenarios、Personas（情境、角色） | Lead Generation、Lead Qualification |
| **Solutions** | 该**行业**能获得什么**业务结果**？ | Business value、ROI（业务价值） | Real estate、Private equity → 见 [openmart-solutions.md](./openmart-solutions.md) |

**Use Cases 核心**：仅包含**情境/场景**（scenario）——「我在做什么任务时用」；**不包含**行业/结果——后者属于 Solutions。

### 1. Lead Generation

**URL**: /use-case/lead-generation  
**Title**: Local Business Lead Generation | Find SMB Leads in Minutes | OpenMart  
**Meta Description**: Get verified local business contacts for lead generation. 200M+ records, 97–99% accuracy, owner finder, data enrichment. Replace manual search with AI-powered SMB prospecting.

**目标关键词**：lead generation, local business lead generation, SMB lead generation, B2B lead generation

#### Headline

Local Business Lead Generation—Verified Contacts in Minutes

Find and reach local business decision makers without manual Google Maps or LinkedIn search. 200M+ verified records, direct owner contacts, 97–99% accuracy.

#### The Problem

- **Manual search is slow**：Scraping Google Maps or searching LinkedIn takes hours per list
- **Outdated contacts**：Bounced emails and disconnected numbers waste time and hurt deliverability
- **No decision-maker access**：Getting past staff to reach owners is tough
- **Poor qualification**：Without revenue, size, or tech stack, you waste time on poor-fit prospects

#### How OpenMart Helps

- **200M+ pre-built database**：Restaurant database, dentist database, plumber leads—300+ categories, filter by location
- **Owner Finder**：Direct owner phone and email, verified in real-time
- **40+ data points**：Revenue estimates, employee count, tech stack, buying signals—qualify before outreach
- **Export to CSV/Excel**：Integrate with CRM, HubSpot, Salesforce, Clay

### 2. Lead Qualification

**URL**: /use-case/lead-qualification  
**Title**: SMB Lead Qualification | Qualify Local Business Leads Before Outreach | OpenMart  
**Meta Description**: Qualify SMB leads with 40+ data points. Revenue estimates, tech stack, buying signals. Filter by size, industry, location. Reduce wasted outreach.

**目标关键词**：lead qualification, SMB prospect list, qualify leads

#### Headline

Qualify SMB Leads Before You Reach Out

Stop wasting time on poor-fit prospects. Use revenue estimates, employee count, tech stack, and buying signals to build targeted lists that convert.

#### The Problem

- **Generic lists**：Contact lists without firmographic data lead to low response rates
- **No revenue visibility**：Can't prioritize high-value accounts
- **Tech stack unknown**：Selling POS, software, or services without knowing current systems
- **Buying signals missed**：New openings, hiring, expansion—hard to spot manually

#### How OpenMart Helps

- **40+ enrichment fields**：Revenue range, employee count, tech stack, recent hiring
- **Buying intent data**：New business alerts, expansion signals
- **Filter & segment**：By category, location, size, revenue
- **Data enrichment**：Verify and enrich existing lists
