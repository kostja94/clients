### 1. Real estate

**URL**: /solutions/real-estate  
**Title**: Local Business Data for Real Estate | Property & Tenant Intelligence | OpenMart  
**Meta Description**: Property data and local business intelligence for real estate. 200M+ verified records, owner contacts, tenant leads. Identify opportunities, conduct due diligence, reach decision makers.

**目标关键词**：local business data real estate, property data, tenant leads, real estate lead generation

#### Headline

Property & Tenant Intelligence for Real Estate

Access 200M+ verified local business records to identify property opportunities, conduct due diligence, and reach tenant decision makers—with owner contacts, revenue estimates, and expansion signals.

#### Challenge

- **Manual research is slow**：Finding tenant leads, property data, and owner contacts takes hours of Google and county records search
- **Outdated tenant lists**：Bounced emails and disconnected numbers waste broker time
- **No firmographic data**：Can't qualify tenants by revenue, size, or growth signals
- **Due diligence gaps**：Missing business intelligence for investment and acquisition decisions

#### Solution

200M+ pre-built database with 30+ property-relevant categories. Owner Finder delivers direct owner/manager contacts. 40+ enrichment fields: revenue estimates, employee count, tech stack, new openings. Filter by location, category, size. Export to CSV for CRM, deal flow tools.

#### Proof

- **97–99% data accuracy** verified across sources
- **40+ data points** per business for qualification
- **Representative clients**：Clipboard Health、Alibaba、Whatnot、Snackpass
- **Coverage**：US、Canada、Australia、New Zealand、Puerto Rico

#### Features Used

- **Local Business Database** — 200M+ records, 300+ categories, /data
- **Owner Finder** — Direct owner phone and email
- **Data Enrichment** — Revenue, size, tech stack, buying signals

*详见 [openmart-features.md](./openmart-features.md)*

### 2. Private equity

**URL**: /solutions/private-equity  
**Title**: Local Business Data for Private Equity | Investment Targets & Due Diligence | OpenMart  
**Meta Description**: PE investment targets and due diligence data. 200M+ local businesses, owner contacts, revenue estimates. Identify acquisition targets, conduct market research, validate portfolio companies.

**目标关键词**：local business data PE, investment targets, due diligence data, PE deal sourcing

#### Headline

Investment Targets & Due Diligence Data for Private Equity

Identify acquisition targets, conduct market research, and validate portfolio companies with 200M+ verified local business records—owner contacts, revenue estimates, and expansion signals.

#### Challenge

- **Deal sourcing is manual**：Finding acquisition targets across regions and categories is time-consuming
- **Limited firmographic data**：Public records lack revenue, size, tech stack for qualification
- **Owner access is hard**：Getting past gatekeepers to reach decision makers delays deal flow
- **Market validation**：Need local business density, growth signals for market sizing

#### Solution

Filter 200M+ records by category, location, revenue range, employee count. Owner Finder for direct owner contacts. Monitoring for new openings and expansion signals. Export for deal flow, CRM, market research.

#### Proof

- **200M+ records** across 200+ countries, 500+ categories
- **97–99% accuracy**；multi-source verification
- **40+ enrichment fields** for qualification

#### Features Used

- **Local Business Database** — /data, /data/{category}
- **Data Enrichment** — Revenue, size, tech stack
- **Monitoring** — New business alerts, expansion signals
