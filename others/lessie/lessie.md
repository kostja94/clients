## 1. Product Overview

**One-line description**:
```
Lessie AI is the world's first People Search AI Agent—an agentic search engine that finds anyone (influencers, B2B leads, investors, talent, partners, coaches, distributors) through natural language, scans 100+ data sources, and automates discovery, scoring, and outreach. Lessie operates as an AI agent that maintains context across the workflow, learns from outcomes, and executes the full "find—list—reach" flow autonomously.
```

**Category**: B2B SaaS / AI People Search / Lead Generation / Influencer Marketing / Recruiting  
**Business model**: Freemium + 订阅（Basic / Pro / Max）  
**Pricing**: Basic $34/月（500 credits、1 并发）；Pro $135/月（2,000 credits、3 并发）；Max $254/月（4,500 credits、5 并发）

**产品形态**：
- **Web App**：lessie.ai（主站）、app.lessie.ai（应用）、**profile.lessie.ai**（Profile Directory）、**lists.lessie.ai**（List Directory）
- **入口**：Platform、Resources、Pricing
- **核心产品线**：Find Influencer、Find Client、Find Investor、Find Talent、Find Partner、Find Coach、Find Distributor

**数据规模**：50M+ 网红、300K+ 候选人、100+ 数据源、95% 联系人准确率  
**公司**：SUPERLINEAR TECHNOLOGY PTE. LTD.（新加坡）

## 2. Positioning Statement

> **For** marketers, sales teams, recruiters, investors, and coaches **who** need to find anyone (influencers, B2B leads, talent, partners, investors) without manual LinkedIn and Google search, **our** Lessie AI **is the** world's first People Search AI Agent **that** understands natural language, scans 100+ data sources, and automates discovery, scoring, and outreach—from search to contact in one flow. **Where** database-based tools require manual filtering and step-by-step extraction, **we** maintain context throughout the workflow, learn from outcomes (who replied, who booked), and execute the full "find—list—reach" flow autonomously—so you describe who you want in plain English and get verified contacts with 3x reply rates.

## 4. Target Audience / ICP

**Primary ICP**:
- **Who**：营销/增长团队、销售/BD、招聘/HR、投资/FA、教练/顾问
- **Industry**：营销、电商、联盟营销、招聘、投资、合作拓展
- **Jobs to be done**：找网红/KOL、B2B 获客、人才 sourcing、找投资人、找合作伙伴、找教练
- **Pain points**：手动 LinkedIn/Google 搜索耗时、多平台切换、无自动化触达、联系人准确率低
- **Buying triggers**：需要规模化找人、需要自动化 outreach、需要替代手动搜索

**Secondary ICP**：代理商、活动组织者、播客主、自由职业者

**Use Case Personas**（产品线对应）：详见 [lessie-use-cases.md](./lessie-use-cases.md)
