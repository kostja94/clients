## 1. Product Overview

**One-line description**:
```
Final Round AI is the #1 real-time AI interview assistant for job seekers—providing live AI help during interviews (Interview Copilot™), AI mock interviews for practice, and detailed post-interview feedback. 100% invisible and undetectable during screen sharing. 10M+ users worldwide（官方宣称）.
```

**Category**: B2C / AI Interview Assistant / Job Search / Career Tech / Recruitment Tech  
**Business model**: Freemium（免费计划 + 付费订阅）  
**Pricing**: 免费计划（有限功能）；付费订阅 $29–$99/月起（Starter $29/月、Pro $49/月、Premium $99/月；以官网为准）；详见 [finalround-features.md](./finalround-features.md) §定价

**产品形态**：
- **Web + Desktop App**：finalroundai.com（主站）、桌面应用（Stealth Mode / Interview Copilot）
- **入口**：Get Started、Download Now、Subscription
- **核心产品**：Interview Copilot（实时面试助手）、AI Mock Interview（模拟面试）、AI Resume Builder、AI Job Hunter

**数据规模**：10M+ 用户（官方宣称）、80+ 国家、91 种语言与口音、10k+ 成功拿到 offer  
**公司**：Final Round AI, Inc.；总部 Palo Alto, CA（456 University Ave）；2025 年 1 月完成 $6.88M Seed 融资；创始人 Jay Ma、Michael Guan（2023 年创立）

## 2. Positioning Statement

> **For** job seekers preparing for technical, behavioral, and case-based interviews **who** want to answer clearly under pressure without rambling or second-guessing, **our** Final Round AI **is the** #1 real-time AI interview assistant **that** runs 100% invisibly during live interviews—delivering structured answers in seconds, personalized to your resume and job details. **Unlike** generic interview prep tools, **we** provide live AI help during the actual interview (Interview Copilot™), AI mock interviews for practice, and actionable post-interview feedback—so you ace every round with confidence.

---

## 3. Value Proposition & Key Messages

- **Primary value prop**：**实时 AI 面试助手**——面试中实时获取结构化答案，100% 隐形、不可被察觉；结合模拟面试与面试后反馈，全流程覆盖。
- **Key messages**:
  - "Ace Your Next Interview Without The Stress."
  - "#1 Real-time AI Interview Assistant for Job Seekers."
  - "100% Invisible & Undetectable" during live interviews and screen sharing.
  - "Answer Clearly Under Pressure" — AI listens, understands context, delivers structured answers in seconds.
  - "Get Better After Every Interview" — see what was asked, how you responded, what to improve.
  - "Answers That Sound Like You" — generated from your resume and job details.
- **Proof points**：10M+ 用户（官方宣称）、10k+ jobs secured、3.6 Trustpilot 评分、80+ 国家、91 种语言/口音；用户来自 Google、Meta、Microsoft、Amazon、OpenAI、SpaceX、Netflix

---

## 4. Target Audience / ICP

**Primary ICP**:
- **Who**：求职者（技术、咨询、金融、营销、销售、医疗、运营等）
- **Industry**：科技、咨询、金融、营销、销售、医疗、运营
- **Jobs to be done**：通过技术/行为/案例面试、提升回答质量、减少紧张与 rambling
- **Pain points**：面试紧张、回答冗长无结构、临场发挥差、缺乏即时反馈
- **Buying triggers**：大厂/高薪面试、需要实时帮助、需要模拟练习、需要面试后复盘

**Secondary ICP**：应届生、转行者、被裁员后重新求职者

**Use Case Personas**（产品线对应）：详见 [finalround-use-cases.md](./finalround-use-cases.md)

**Language / locale**：英文为主；支持 91 种语言与口音；用户遍布 80+ 国家
