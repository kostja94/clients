## What Is Parakeet AI?

Parakeet AI (parakeet-ai.com) is an AI Interview Assistant that provides real-time support during live interviews. It automatically detects questions and generates answers using top-tier LLMs (GPT-5, GPT-4.1, Claude 4.0 Sonnet). The product emphasizes 100% privacy and undetectability—invisible on screen share, in the dock, and in the task manager.

Parakeet AI supports behavioral, technical, and coding interviews. It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Amazon Chime, Webex, HackerRank, LeetCode, and phone calls (via Google Voice). You can upload your resume and supporting documents for personalized answers. The company claims 1.5M+ users, 4.86/5 from 340K+ reviews, and #1 Interview Assistant by website visits (SimilarWeb).

## Key Features

### 100% Private and Undetectable

Parakeet AI claims to be invisible on screen share, in the dock, in the task manager, and to tab switching. The product operates at the system level and states it is undetectable by proctoring software and screen-sharing tools. For Zoom, the FAQ recommends enabling "Advanced capture with window filtering" in Zoom settings to ensure Parakeet AI stays hidden during screen sharing.

### Blazing Fast Transcription

Parakeet AI uses a state-of-the-art transcription model for highly accurate, fast transcription. Auto-detect identifies questions and auto-generates answers so you stay prepared in real time.

### Multi-LLM Choice

You can choose between GPT-5, GPT-4.1, and Claude 4.0 Sonnet—the best LLMs available—to tailor answer quality and style to your preference.

### Full Coding Interview Support

Parakeet AI supports coding interviews by listening for coding questions and capturing the screen when LeetCode-style questions are shared. It provides code suggestions and explanations during live coding rounds.

### Resume & Documents

Upload your resume once for instant interview answers matched to your experience. You can also upload supporting documents (e.g., project portfolios) that Parakeet AI references during the call.

### AI Notes After Each Call

After each call, Parakeet AI automatically generates notes—key points, action items, and decisions—so nothing is lost.

### Auto-Detect Meetings

For subscriptions, Parakeet AI can run in the background and auto-detect meetings, prompting you to turn it on when a meeting starts.

### Mobile & Desktop

Parakeet AI has a mobile-optimized browser version (no app store download). You can run desktop and mobile simultaneously with a single session. Phone calls are supported via Google Voice.

## Who Parakeet AI Is For (And Who It's Not)

**Best for**: Candidates who want LLM choice (GPT vs Claude), desktop-first privacy (invisible in dock/task manager), post-call AI notes, and a large user base with strong reviews. It suits those who value 30-day money-back guarantee and unlimited calls on subscription.

**Not ideal for**: Users on a tight budget (higher price point than many competitors); those who need integrated mock interview, resume builder, or auto apply in one product; or candidates who prefer a vendor with broader platform support (e.g., CodeSignal, Mercor) in a unified flow.
