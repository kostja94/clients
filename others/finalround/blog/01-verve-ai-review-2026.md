## What Is Verve AI?

Verve AI (vervecopilot.com) is an AI Interview Copilot that provides real-time support during live interviews. It transcribes conversations, detects questions, and suggests expert-level answers tailored to your resume, job description, and company. The product offers both browser and desktop app access, with Stealth Mode in the desktop app to keep the Copilot invisible during full-screen or app-based screen sharing.

Verve AI supports behavioral, technical, live coding, case study, and online assessment interviews. It works with Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Amazon Chime, and includes specialized copilots for HireVue, domain knowledge, and mobile phone interviews. The company claims 50,000+ candidates and targets FAANG, big tech, and quant roles.

## Key Features

### Stealth Mode

Stealth Mode keeps the Copilot hidden from screen sharing. Available in the desktop app, it ensures the AI overlay is visible only to you—even during full-screen or app-based sharing. Browser tab sharing is designed to stay discreet without Stealth Mode. Desktop app also supports hotkey screen capture, split screen mode, and hide-from-dock for maximum discretion.

### Live Transcription & Auto Question Detection

Verve AI transcribes interviews in real time and automatically detects questions to deliver instant, precise answers. This reduces the need to manually trigger help and helps you respond quickly under pressure.

### Personalized AI Copilot

The Copilot uses your resume, job description, and company context to tailor suggestions. It highlights skills and experience that align with company values and structures responses for behavioral and case questions. You can create an interview profile, add documents, and test in a Playground before your interview.

### Coding Interview Copilot

A screen capture plugin captures coding questions directly from your screen and generates solutions in real time. This supports live coding interviews on platforms like CoderPad, LeetCode, and HackerRank.

### Online Assessment Copilot

Separate from the Coding Copilot, the OA Copilot provides one-click support for timed coding assessments and technical tests.

### 12 Domain Copilots

Verve AI offers 12 specialized domain copilots trained across 25 industries—including Software Engineer, Product, Data, Consulting, Finance, and Marketing—with tailored job skills and interview questions.

### AI Mock Interview & AI Resume Editor

Verve AI includes an AI Mock Interview with immersive simulation and extensive question banks, plus an AI Resume Editor that generates ATS-optimized resumes.

### Interview Report & Question Banks

Post-session reports and a 3,000+ question bank help you practice and review. You can train the model with custom materials for role-specific or industry-specific interviews.

### Multi-Platform & Multi-Language

Verve AI works across browser, desktop, and mobile. It supports 25+ languages and integrates with major meeting and assessment platforms.

## Who Verve AI Is For (And Who It's Not)

**Best for**: Candidates preparing for FAANG or big tech roles who want unlimited live and mock sessions, strong coding support, and multi-language coverage. It suits those who prefer a lower price point with no session limits and value Stealth Mode in the desktop app.

**Not ideal for**: Users who need a full job-search flow (auto apply, LinkedIn optimization) in one product, or those who prioritize a single vendor with 10M+ users and broader platform support (e.g., LeetCode, HackerRank, CodeSignal) in a unified experience.
