## 二、核心功能详情

### 1. Interview Copilot™（实时面试助手）

**核心关键词**：interview copilot, real-time AI interview help, AI interview assistant, live interview AI

**扩展关键词**：invisible interview AI, stealth interview help, undetectable interview AI, real-time interview help during screen share, AI interview help Google Meet, real-time interview help Zoom, AI interview Microsoft Teams, AI interview LeetCode, coding interview copilot, HackerRank interview AI, CodeSignal interview help, technical interview help, behavioral interview AI, case interview AI, system design interview AI

**核心卖点**：
- **100% Invisible & Undetectable**：面试中静默运行，屏幕共享时面试官不可见；无弹窗、无可见界面
- **实时结构化答案**：听到问题后数秒内生成结构化回答；基于简历与职位信息个性化
- **多平台支持**：Google Meet、Zoom、Microsoft Teams、LeetCode、Mercor、HackerRank、CodeSignal、CoderPad、CodePen 等
- **Answers That Sound Like You**：根据简历与职位生成，听起来具体、可信、资深，而非泛泛而谈
- **桌面应用**：需下载桌面应用启用 Stealth Mode；检测到会议时启动 Stealth Copilot

**使用流程**：上传简历 → 选择目标职位 → 下载桌面应用 → 面试时启动 Stealth Copilot → 实时获取答案

### 2. AI Mock Interview（模拟面试）

**核心关键词**：AI mock interview, interview practice AI, mock interview AI

**扩展关键词**：interview practice, AI interview prep, behavioral interview AI, STAR method AI, coding interview practice, AI mock interview for coding, technical interview practice, case interview practice, AI mock interview tech, HireVue interview prep, one-way video interview AI

**核心卖点**：
- 模拟真实面试场景；角色与行业定制
- 即时反馈：回答后立即获得改进建议
- 覆盖技术、行为、案例、系统设计等面试类型
- 问题来自真实面试趋势，非泛泛题库

### 3. Interview Report（面试报告）

**核心关键词**：interview feedback, post-interview analysis, interview report

**扩展关键词**：post-interview feedback, interview score, interview improvement, interview performance report, AI interview feedback

**核心卖点**：
- 面试后查看：问了什么、你怎么回答、下次如何改进
- AI 改进建议、语音模式、表达清晰度、参与度分析
- 面试评分卡（如 98 分总分）

### 4. AI Resume Builder

**核心关键词**：AI resume builder, resume optimizer

**扩展关键词**：ATS resume, resume keywords, resume checker AI, ATS resume checker, resume optimization AI

**核心卖点**：AI 简历优化、ATS 兼容检查、关键词推荐

### 5. AI Job Hunter / Auto Apply

**核心关键词**：AI job hunter, auto apply jobs

**扩展关键词**：auto apply, job application AI, bulk apply jobs, AI job search, automated job application

**核心卖点**：按条件自动投递职位；从申请到 offer 全流程支持

### 6. LinkedIn Profile Optimizer

**核心关键词**：LinkedIn profile optimizer, LinkedIn optimization

**扩展关键词**：LinkedIn headline optimizer, LinkedIn profile AI, LinkedIn profile for recruiters

**核心卖点**：AI 分析 Profile、headline 优化、行业关键词建议、实时 AI 反馈、summary 与 experience 增强建议

### 7. LinkedIn to Resume / AI Resume Builder

**核心关键词**：LinkedIn to resume, LinkedIn resume builder, AI resume builder

**扩展关键词**：convert LinkedIn to resume, ATS resume, resume optimizer

**核心卖点**：从 LinkedIn 导出并优化简历；ATS 兼容；关键词推荐

### 8. AI Salary Calculator

**核心关键词**：AI salary calculator, salary negotiation

**扩展关键词**：market value, salary negotiation AI, compensation negotiation

**核心卖点**：市场价值评估；薪资谈判支持

### 9. Interview Questions Bank

**核心关键词**：interview questions bank, interview questions by company

**扩展关键词**：real company interview questions, tech interview questions

**核心卖点**：真实公司面试题；按公司/角色筛选

### 10. Phone Interview Copilot（待建，竞品参考 LockedIn AI）

**核心关键词**：phone interview AI, phone interview copilot

**扩展关键词**：AI interview help on phone, dual-device interview copilot, phone interview prep, interview copilot on phone

**竞品参考**（LockedIn AI /phone-interview）：手机浏览器访问、无需安装；双设备（电脑面试+手机听+手机收建议）完全不可察觉；Structured Talking Points、Interview Frameworks、Confidence Prompts、Fast Prep Refresh；支持 In-person、Video、Technical、Meeting。
