## Quick Tips for Family Organizer Success

1. **One app, one truth** — Resist the urge to keep a separate calendar or list. Put everything in the family organizer.
2. **Capture at the moment** — When a flyer, email, or reminder arrives, add it immediately. Voice and photo input make this fast.
3. **Review weekly** — Spend 5–10 minutes each week reviewing the schedule and meal plan. Catch conflicts early.
4. **Involve kids** — Older kids can add their own events or check the calendar. Reduces "I didn't know" moments.
5. **Sync everywhere** — Ensure the app syncs with Google, Apple, or Outlook so events appear on everyone's devices.

## Migration: Switching from Cozi or FamilyWall

**From Cozi**: Cozi exports to CSV or syncs with Google Calendar. If you use Google sync, your events may already be in Google—Nori can connect to that. For lists and meal plans, you'll need to recreate. Start with the calendar; migrate lists over a few weeks.

**From FamilyWall**: Export your calendar if the app supports it. Add to Google Calendar, then connect Nori. Tasks and location features don't transfer—recreate in the new app. Location sharing: use Google Maps or Life360 if needed.

**Pro tip**: Don't delete the old app immediately. Run both in parallel for 2–3 weeks. Ensure nothing is missed. Then switch fully.
