## When Import Fails: Fallbacks

**Link returns nothing** — The site may block scrapers. Try screenshotting the recipe and uploading. Or copy-paste the key parts manually. Some sites use unusual formatting that parsers miss.

**Screenshot is incomplete** — Ensure ingredients and instructions are visible. Scroll and capture multiple screenshots if needed. Combine into one image or upload separately—some apps support multi-image import.

**Handwritten scan has errors** — OCR struggles with messy handwriting. Review and fix. For critical recipes (grandma's secret), type the key parts manually. Better to have it right than fast.
