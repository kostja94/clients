## When Trip Planning Fails

**Confirmation emails don't parse** — Some emails have unusual formats. Forward anyway; the tool may extract something. If not, add manually. Or screenshot the key details and use photo-to-calendar if your app supports it. The goal is capture—use whatever method works.

**Multiple events in one email** — A single email may have flight, hotel, and activity. The tool may extract one or all. Review. Add missing events manually. Email-to-calendar is a starting point; verification ensures nothing is missed.

**Time zone confusion** — "8am local" vs. "8am home time" matters. Double-check flight times. When you travel, switch your device time zone—or the app may auto-detect. Don't miss a flight because of a time zone error.

**Last-minute changes** — Flight delayed. Hotel overbooked. Update the event in the app. The shared calendar syncs. Everyone sees the change. The system holds the latest; you just update it. Call alerts will use the updated time if you've set them.
