## Why Meal-Plan-to-Grocery Matters

When you plan meals for the week, you know what ingredients you need. An app that **auto-generates** a grocery list from your meal plan saves time and reduces "what did we need?" stress. You pick meals; the app breaks down ingredients, deduplicates (e.g., "2 cups flour" from two recipes becomes one line), and creates the list. No more flipping through recipes with a pen. Nori does this automatically; Paprika and Cozi require manual list building from your plan.

**The manual alternative**: Without auto-generation, you open each recipe, write down ingredients, combine duplicates, and hope you didn't miss anything. For 5–7 meals, that's 15–10 minutes. With auto-generation, it's seconds. The list is ready when you are.

**Staples and extras**: Auto-generated lists cover meal ingredients. But you also need staples—milk, bread, paper towels. Voice input fills the gap: "Add milk to grocery" or "Add dish soap." The item goes to the shared list. No typing. No "I'll add it when I get to the store" (which often means forgetting).
