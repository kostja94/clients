## Migration Tips: Switching from Cozi

**Before you switch:**
1. Export or screenshot key lists (shopping, to-dos) if the new app doesn't import from Cozi
2. Note your recurring events—you may need to recreate them
3. If Cozi syncs to Google Calendar, your events may already be there—the new app can sync with Google and pull them in

**Transition period:** Some families run both apps for a week or two. Add new events to the new app; reference Cozi for existing data until you've migrated.

**What you'll gain:** AI input (voice, photo, email), AI meal planning, and often a simpler flow for adding events. What you might miss: Cozi's premade lists (Gold), agenda emails, and familiar interface. The tradeoff is usually worth it if manual entry is your main pain point.

**Week 1**: Add new events to the new app. Reference Cozi for existing data. Run both in parallel. Get the new app's workflow down. Test voice, photo, email. See how fast it is.

**Week 2:** Migrate key lists. Shopping, to-dos, meal plan. Recreate in the new app. If Cozi syncs to Google Calendar, your events may already be there—connect the new app to Google and pull them in.
