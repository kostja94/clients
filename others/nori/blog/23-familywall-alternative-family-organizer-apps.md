## Why Families Switch from FamilyWall

**Price** — FamilyWall charges from $4.99/month. Cozi, Nori, and TimeTree offer free tiers. For budget-conscious families, a free alternative can save $60+/year.

**Simpler needs** — Some families don't need location or messaging. They just want a shared calendar. Calendara and OurCal offer simpler, calendar-focused alternatives.

**Co-parenting** — OurCal and Nori are strong for co-parenting. Shared custody, two households, one schedule. FamilyWall works, but alternatives may fit better for specific co-parenting workflows.

## Migration from FamilyWall: What to Expect

FamilyWall users switching to Nori gain AI input (voice, photo, email) and meal planning—but lose location tracking and in-app messaging. If location is essential, consider keeping FamilyWall or using <a href="https://maps.google.com/" rel="nofollow noopener">Google Maps</a> location sharing alongside your new calendar app. For messaging, use your existing family group chat (WhatsApp, iMessage, etc.). The tradeoff: more automation for less built-in location/messaging.

**Step 1: Export your data** — FamilyWall may allow export of events and lists. Check the app settings. Export to CSV or sync with Google Calendar if you've connected it.

**Step 2: Connect your new app** — Nori, Cozi, and others sync with Google/Apple/Outlook. If FamilyWall pushed events to Google Calendar, they may already appear when you connect.

**Step 3: Recreate lists and tasks** — If the new app doesn't import from FamilyWall, manually recreate key lists. Start with the most important: shopping, chores, recurring tasks.

**Step 4: Transition period** — Use both apps for a week if needed. Add new events to the new app; reference FamilyWall for existing data until you've migrated.
