## Tips for Best Photo Extraction

**Lighting**: Avoid shadows. Ensure text is readable. Overhead light or natural light works best.

**Angle**: Shoot straight on. Avoid skew—lay the flyer flat. Crooked photos can confuse OCR.

**Full frame**: Include the entire flyer. Don't crop important details. If the flyer is large, take multiple photos of sections.

**Screenshots**: For digital flyers (team apps, school portals), screenshot the full schedule. Upload like a photo. Same process.

**Recurring events**: Sports schedules often say "every Tuesday 4:30 pm." The app may detect this. Verify the end date—fall soccer might end in November. Edit if needed.

## When Photo Extraction Fails

**Handwritten notes** — OCR and AI work best with typed or printed text. Handwritten flyers may have lower accuracy. Use voice instead: "Add field trip March 15." Or type manually. Don't rely on photo for critical handwritten content.

**Complex tables** — Multi-column schedules with many events. The app may extract some but miss others. Review carefully. Add missing events manually. Photo is a starting point, not a replacement for verification.

**Poor lighting or angle** — Blurry or skewed photos confuse OCR. Retake with good lighting, flat surface, full frame. The better the photo, the better the extraction.

**Multiple pages** — A long PDF or multi-page flyer. Screenshot or photograph each page. Upload each. The app extracts from each image. Combine into a coherent calendar. Or forward the PDF via email if your app supports email-to-calendar (Nori does).
