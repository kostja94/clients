## When Meal Planning Fails

**Nobody follows the plan** — Life happens. Meetings run late. Kids get sick. The plan is a guide, not a contract. Swap meals. Use leftovers. Order takeout. The goal is reducing decisions, not perfection. Adjust as you go.

**Grocery list too long** — Plan fewer meals. Or use fridge-to-recipe: snap what you have, get suggestions. Reduce waste. Reduce shopping. The list should serve you, not overwhelm you.

**Allergy filter missed something** — Always verify ingredients. AI filters help but aren't perfect. Read every recipe before cooking. For severe allergies, double-check.
