## 1. Product Overview

**One-line description**:
```
Hologrow is an AI-powered intent intelligence platform for e-commerce that decodes visitor purchase intent in real-time with 94% accuracy, tracks 200+ micro-behaviors in under 100ms, and converts browsers into buyers through personalized experiences—so you turn buyer intent into revenue.
```

**Category**: B2B SaaS / E-commerce AI / Intent Intelligence / Conversion Optimization / Personalization  
**Business model**: 定制定价（Book a Demo 获取报价）  
**Pricing**: 定制；需联系销售

**产品形态**：
- **Web App**：hologrow.ai（主站、落地页）
- **Shopify App**：apps.shopify.com/hologrow（一键安装）
- **入口**：Book a Demo、Get Shopify App
- **核心产品线**：Intent AI Sales Agent、Intent Intelligence（Intent Recognition、Intent Dashboard、Buyer Intent Report Generator）、Loyalty Program Generator

**数据规模**：500M+ 访客会话分析、94% 购买意图准确率、<100ms 检测、200+ 微行为追踪  
**代表客户**：Meeden、LIVTAB、nitetronic、Ikarao、BABESIDE

## 2. Positioning Statement

> **For** e-commerce brands, DTC sellers, and Shopify merchants **who** struggle with anonymous traffic, cart abandonment, and short attention windows, **our** Hologrow **is an** AI intent intelligence platform **that** decodes visitor purchase intent in real-time with 94% accuracy, tracks 200+ micro-behaviors in under 100ms, and converts browsers into buyers through personalized experiences. **Unlike** traditional analytics that show what happened, **we** reveal what's about to happen—and empower you to influence it—**because** we're built to turn buyer intent into revenue with AI-powered personalization.

## 4. Target Audience / ICP

**Primary ICP**:
- **Who**：电商品牌、独立站、DTC 品牌、Shopify 商家、营销/增长团队
