## 1. Product Overview

**One-line**（首页，2026-06-02）:
```
Hellyeah is the AI-native CLI for building, testing, and scaling growth campaigns from one command layer — agents research, create, launch, and learn across your ad, lifecycle, and content stack.
```

**Category**: B2B / Enterprise **AI growth infrastructure** + **digital growth workers**（SDK / CLI / agents）  
**商业**: `/demo`（15 min）；Contact；**AIMA 免费层 $0**（WhatsApp）；企业 **Forward-Deployed Growth Pod**（按托管 spend 比例，经 demo 申请）  
**形态**: [hellyeahai.com](https://www.hellyeahai.com/) — Manifesto、Customers、Capabilities、Solutions、Arena

**叙事层次**:
1. **主站**：*npm install your growth engine*；RCLL = Research → Create → Launch → Learn  
2. **About**：AI-native growth infrastructure for **digital workers**  
3. **AIMA 子品牌**：*The marketing team your business never had* — chat/WhatsApp 向  
4. **Manifesto**：AIMA sees · Mutation reacts · Forge builds · Déjà Vu remembers

## 2. Positioning Statement

> **For** growth, marketing, and revenue leaders **who** need agents to **operate** paid, GEO, lifecycle, and creator workflows—not another dashboard—**our** Hellyeah **is** the command layer (CLI, SDK, AIMA) **that** runs Research–Create–Launch–Learn with spend caps, approvals, and growth memory. **Unlike** agencies or rule-based automation, **we** compound experiments and real-time intelligence (**Mutation**, **Forge**, **Déjà Vu**).
