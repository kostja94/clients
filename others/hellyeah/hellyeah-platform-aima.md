## 7. 定价（线上）

### AIMA · Free — $0 forever

- Unlimited brands / channels / creative / campaigns  
- Lifecycle · email · SMS · WhatsApp  
- Brand-safety + memory + reporting  
- WhatsApp interface · Community support  
- **Spend through AIMA, not to AIMA**（广告费付给各平台）
