## Conclusion 多样结尾示例

每篇 conclusion 用不同的收束方式（避免跨篇模板化）：

**预测型**：
> The next few years will decide whether organic growth stays a manual guessing game or becomes a discipline with its own tooling. The teams that treat research as part of the production pipeline — not a pre-launch luxury — are the ones whose month four doesn't look like a repeat of month one.

**未解问题型**：
> Whether any system can fully decode the taste that separates a viral hit from a near-miss remains an open question. For now, the practical answer is a loop: watch more, decode better, produce faster, and let the wins compound — the machine proposes the patterns, the team supplies the judgment.

**具体警告型**：
> Don't confuse a single viral video with a growth system. A one-off hit that your team can't explain is a data point, not a strategy. If you can't name which variable you'd run again next week, you haven't built a process — you've had a good week.

**行动号召型（仅 Product/Scenario 适用）**：
> The five-engine loop from signal detection to compounding growth isn't a marketing diagram — it's the order the workflow actually runs. The hard part isn't learning the steps. It's trusting the research enough to commit next week's shoot list to it.
