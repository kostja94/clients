## Conclusion 多样结尾示例

每篇 conclusion 用不同的收束方式（避免跨篇模板化）：

**预测型**：
> As community AI evolves from simple auto-replies to judgment-based engagement, the line between "support tool" and "community teammate" will continue to blur. The teams that treat AI as a member of the team — not just a cost-saving switch — will see the biggest gains.

**未解问题型**：
> Whether AI-driven onboarding can match the warmth of a human welcome message for tight-knit communities under 100 members remains an open question. For now, the sweet spot is large, fast-moving communities where speed matters more than personal touch.

**具体警告型**：
> Don't confuse auto-reply with call deflection. Auto-reply answers one message. Call deflection changes the entire support workflow — from who answers first to what gets escalated. If your tool only does the former, you're not deflecting calls. You're just routing them differently.

**行动号召型（仅 Product/Scenario 适用）**：
> The 5-minute setup isn't a marketing line — it's the actual time from connecting your Discord server to seeing the first AI answer land in #support. The hard part isn't the setup. It's trusting the AI enough to let go of the keyboard.
