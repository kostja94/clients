# What Should You Look For in an App Builder Tool?

The best app builder depends on what you're trying to accomplish. You can clarify with a few questions:

  * Do you need a complete application or just specific components?
  * Are you prototyping ideas or building for production?
  * How much technical expertise does your team have?
  * What's your timeline—days or months?
  * Do you want to manage multiple tools or one platform?

Once you've understood your goals, you can choose the tool that best suits them. As you evaluate options, consider these essential factors:

  * **Completeness:** Does it build the entire stack or just pieces?
  * **Integration effort:** How much manual wiring between components?
  * **Deployment:** Can you ship to production easily or need DevOps expertise?
  * **Iteration speed:** How fast can you make changes and test?
  * **Code ownership:** Can you export and modify the generated code?
  * **Cost structure:** Total cost including tools, hosting, and maintenance?
