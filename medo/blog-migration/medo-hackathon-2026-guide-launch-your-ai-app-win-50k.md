# 🛠️ Project Requirements

## What You Need to Build

You must create a **working application using MeDo** and submit it under one of the following tracks:

  * **Work & Productivity**  
Tools that improve efficiency (e.g., task managers, automation tools, dashboards) 
  * **Business & E-commerce**  
Solutions for real business needs (e.g., CRM, online stores, booking systems) 
  * **Lifestyle & Game**  
Fun or creative apps (e.g., games, hobby trackers, creative tools) 
  * **Learning & Education**  
Apps for learning and skill-building (e.g., tutorials, language tools, quizzes) 
  * **Surprise Us!**  
Anything unique that doesn’t fit the above categories 

## Functionality Matters

Your project must:

  * Be fully functional and stable
  * Run as described in your submission
  * Match what you show in your demo or explain in your description

## Platform Requirements

  * Your app must run on the platform you claim (web, mobile, etc.)
  * Judges should be able to access and test it easily

## New or Updated Projects

  * You can build something **completely new** , or
  * Improve an **existing project**

But if it’s existing, you must show **significant updates made during the hackathon period**

## Third-Party Integrations

  * You can use external tools (APIs, SDKs, data)
  * But you must have the **right to use them**
  * Follow all licensing and usage rules

# 📦 Submission Requirements

## Core Submission Requirements

Your submission must include:

  * A **working project built with MeDo** that meets all project requirements
  * A **publicly accessible URL** to your deployed application
  * A **clear project description** explaining your app’s features and functionality

In your description, be sure to cover:

  * What problem your app solves and why you built it
  * How you structured your conversations with MeDo
  * The most impressive feature or functionality generated with MeDo
  * How you used plugins or APIs (if applicable)

## Language Requirements

All submission materials must be **in English**.If your original content is in another language, you must provide **English translations** for:

  * Your project description
  * Demo video (subtitles or narration)
  * Testing instructions
  * Any other submitted materials

## Demo Video (Optional but Recommended)

You may include a demo video to showcase your project. If you do:

  * Keep it **under 3 minutes**
  * Show your app **working in real use**
  * Upload it publicly to **YouTube, Vimeo, or Youku**
  * Include the video link in your submission
  * Avoid using copyrighted music or third-party trademarks without permission

## Choose Your Track

Make sure to indicate which category (track) your project is submitted under.

# 🧠 Judging Criteria

## Innovation & Creativity

How original is your idea?

  * Does your project introduce something new or unexpected?
  * Are you using MeDo in a creative or unique way?
  * Does your solution stand out from typical approaches?

## Implementation & Technical Depth

How well did you use MeDo?

  * Did you effectively leverage features like:
    * Multi-turn conversations
    * Plugins and integrations
    * Full-stack app generation
  * Does your project show depth or advanced usage of the platform?

## User Experience & Value

How useful and polished is your app?

  * Is it easy to use and intuitive?
  * Does it look clean and appealing?
  * Does it solve a real problem or create meaningful value?

## Additional Award Criteria

### Creative Content Award

Judged based on:

  * Originality and creativity
  * Content quality and storytelling
  * Engagement (likes, shares, comments)

### Community Choice Award

  * Determined by **community votes on Discord**
  * Projects with the most votes win

### Social Blitz Prize

  * Awarded to the **first 50 participants** who:
    * Post about the hackathon on social media
    * Use the hashtag 👉 **#BuiltWithMeDo**
    * Include the link in their Devpost submission

# 🎯 Tips to Win

If you want to stand out in the MeDo Hackathon, focus on what judges actually care about.

### ✅ Build something useful

Solve a real problem or create something people would genuinely use.

Don’t just build the app. Make it clear how MeDo helped you create it, including your prompts, iterations, and key features.
