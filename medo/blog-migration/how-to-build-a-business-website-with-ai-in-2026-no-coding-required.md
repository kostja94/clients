#### **Step 1: Define Your Website's Purpose and Goals**

Before diving into **website design and development** , establish a solid foundation. Ask yourself:

  * **What's the main goal?** Are you selling products (ecommerce website), showcasing a portfolio, or providing service information?
  * **Who's your target audience?** Understand their needs to tailor your website content and user experience
  * **What brand identity do you want to create?** Ensure consistency in colors, fonts, and messaging
  * **How should the site structure be planned?** Organize key pages logically to improve navigation

**Pro Tip** : Conduct competitive analysis to research competitors' **website builder** choices, **SEO strategy** , and unique value propositions.

Depending on your goals, consider these website types:

  * Small business websites
  * Ecommerce websites / Online stores
  * Portfolio websites for creatives
  * SaaS landing pages
  * Blog and content sites
  * ...
