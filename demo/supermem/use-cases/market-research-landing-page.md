## 3. Explain Value（说明价值）

### Problem（痛点）

| 痛点 | 描述 |
|------|------|
| 预算有限 | 请不起 McKinsey/BCG，但需要同等深度的市场洞察 |
| 时间紧迫 | 进入新市场或评估竞品，决策窗口短 |
| 质量不稳定 | 外包/实习生产出参差不齐，不敢用于战略决策 |
| 缺乏框架 | 不知道从哪些维度分析竞品、如何结构化市场研究 |

### Solution（SuperMem 如何解决）

**Research Agent 能力**（由顶级顾问训练）：
- 深度市场研究：市场规模、趋势、进入壁垒
- 竞品分析：功能、定价、定位、优劣势矩阵
- 战略洞察：机会识别、风险提示、建议行动
- 结构化产出：报告、表格、可执行建议，非泛泛建议

**Expert-in-the-loop 工作流**：
1. 你描述研究目标（新市场、竞品、战略问题）
2. Research Agent 产出初稿（分析、报告、洞察）
3. 专家或你本人审批关键结论与建议
4. 审批通过后交付，确保质量与责任归属

**与纯 AI 工具的区别**：
- 训练自真实顾问：prompts、指标、维度由专家沉淀
- 人类审批：关键决策点需你或专家确认
- 真实工作产出：计划、报告、分析，可直接用于决策

## 4. Remove Doubt（消除顾虑）

### FAQ 区块

**Q: How is this different from ChatGPT or Perplexity?**  
A: Research Agent is trained by top consultants—the prompts, frameworks, and analysis dimensions come from real strategic work. You get structured, decision-ready output (reports, competitor matrices) instead of generic answers. Plus, expert-in-the-loop means critical insights are reviewed before delivery.

**Q: What if I need human expertise, not just AI?**  
A: SuperMem is a marketplace. You can hire the expert who trained the Research Agent for a call, or have them review and refine the Agent's output. AI does the heavy lifting; humans keep the judgment.

**Q: How fast can I get a market research report?**  
A: Research Agent can produce initial analysis in days, not months. Timeline depends on scope—narrow competitor analysis is faster; full market entry assessment takes longer. You'll see real-time progress as the Agent works.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Enterprise sovereignty and privacy are core to SuperMem. Your context stays in your control. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.

### 对比锚定（可选）
- vs 咨询公司：1/10 成本，天数级交付
- vs 纯 AI：专家训练 + 人类审批，产出可决策
- vs 外包/实习生：专家级框架，质量稳定
