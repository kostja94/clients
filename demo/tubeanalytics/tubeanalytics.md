## 1. Product summary

**TubeAnalytics** targets **serious creators** with a YouTube growth analytics stack: **authenticated** CPM/RPM, watch time, and retention from the **YouTube Analytics API** (not estimates-only), plus **competitor tracking, trend discovery, thumbnail testing, revenue breakdown, comment inbox, keyword/SEO tools, content calendar, AI ideas & scripts**—from “read the data” to “what to film and how to package it.”

*Features & pricing*: [tubeanalytics-features.md](./tubeanalytics-features.md)

## 2. Trust & compliance (site highlights)

- **OAuth**: no YouTube password; read-only analytics scope (About / FAQ).
- **Transparency**: [Editorial policy](https://www.tubeanalytics.net/editorial-policy), [Methodology](https://www.tubeanalytics.net/methodology) (linked from About).
- **Independence**: footer states **Not affiliated with YouTube or Google LLC**.

---

## 3. Positioning

- **API-grade revenue & retention**: differentiate from “display-only” tools via RPM/CPM and timestamp-level drop-offs.
- **Competitors & trends**: side-by-side channels and niche trends; same buyer as vidIQ/TubeBuddy but site pushes a **modern dashboard** and **less bloated browser extension**.
- **AI workflow**: thumbnail scoring, ideas, viral scripts, comments—late-stage creator pipeline.

---

## 4. ICP (short)

- Mid-size YouTube creators monetizing or aiming to (need RPM & geo splits).
- Multi-channel or schedule-focused creators (calendar, competitor upload rhythm).
- MCNs, agencies, multi-client accounts (Enterprise: white-label, API, account manager).
