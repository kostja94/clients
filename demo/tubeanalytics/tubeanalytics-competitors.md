## 1. Direct competitors (YouTube creator growth)

| Competitor | Positioning | Relationship to TubeAnalytics |
|------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| **vidIQ** | Keywords, competitors, Studio-embedded extension | Site FAQ positions as alternative; vidIQ strong on in-browser workflow & community |
| **TubeBuddy** | A/B tests, SEO, bulk tools | High feature overlap; TubeBuddy long history & extension distribution |
| **YouTube Studio** | Free first-party analytics | Site contrast: **fragmented** UI; lacks unified competitor view & advanced AI workflow |
| **Channel Meter / social-listening style** | Some cross-platform analytics | If YouTube-only depth matters, TubeAnalytics stays more vertical |

**Site differentiation (paraphrased)**: authenticated CPM/RPM from Analytics API; competitors & trends; AI thumbnails / scripts / comments; unified dashboard; less “bloated” browser extension (homepage / FAQ).

## 2. Indirect competitors
