# TubeAnalytics — Use cases

**Last updated**: 2026-04-24

---

## 1. Personas

| Persona | Description | Core need |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| **Growth-focused creator** | ~10K–1M+ subs; weekly or higher publish cadence | Which topics, thumbnails, and publish times lift **RPM and subs** at the margin |
| **Multi-channel operator** | 2+ brands or languages | One view; channel count & history depth within plan tiers |
| **Educational / how-to creator** | Long-form, retention-sensitive | Retention curves to find drop-off minutes; scripts & chapters |
| **MCN / agency** | Manages multiple clients | White-label, API, longer history, account manager (Enterprise) |
| **Migrating from browser tools** | Heavy vidIQ/TubeBuddy-style extension users | Less extension reliance; “dashboard + API-backed data” in one place |

---

## 2. Typical scenarios

1. **Revenue attribution**: Find **20% of videos driving 80% of ad revenue** (site case narrative); schedule and double down on those themes.  
2. **Competitor cadence**: Compare **upload times and day-one views**; adjust your publishing window (homepage creator story).  
3. **Thumbnail decisions**: A/B or AI CTR prediction vs “gut-feel” thumbnail swaps.  
4. **Trend timing**: Act **2–3 weeks** before niche peaks (homepage claim; align with in-product alerts).  
5. **Comment ops**: Filter, reply, moderate in bulk—less tab-hopping across Studio.  
6. **Cold start with trust narrative**: Small channels still OAuth real data; avoid “only for big creators” perception (FAQ: works under 1,000 subs).

---

## 3. Out of scope (boundaries for external copy)

- No video metadata edits or paid campaign management—position as **analytics & decisions**, not MCN ops.  
- Competitor data is **public channel** aggregation; third-party use must follow platform and privacy terms (per site ToS).
