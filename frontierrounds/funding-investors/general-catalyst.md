## 概览

General Catalyst is a global venture firm founded in 2000 that has grown into one of the most aggressive AI deployers among big platforms. It led Mistral's €600M Series B, entered Anthropic's cap table, and led the record US$1.1B River AI round in 2026. Its AI portfolio spans frontier labs, defense AI and healthcare AI.

## 详情

General Catalyst was founded by Joel Cutler and David Fialkow and has backed 800+ companies from seed to growth. The firm has additional offices in San Francisco, New York, London, Berlin, Bangalore and Washington DC, and Jeannette zu Fürstenberg serves as president.

## 投资逻辑

*How General Catalyst Is Playing the AI Cycle*
### AI as a National-resilience and System-change Thesis
GC under Taneja frames AI in system terms — "American leadership in AI urgently requires leadership in open-weight models" (on River AI) — pairing applied-AI bets with a policy voice that most financial VCs avoid. It is an argument the firm is willing to put money behind at the frontier of open weights.

### Healthcare AI as Home Turf
GC's Health Assurance heritage gives it a differentiated lane: Hippocratic AI (safety-first medical LLM, backed since 2023), Formation Bio, Commure and Applied Intuition (autonomy) sit alongside its traditional health portfolio — an AI angle most generalist funds cannot claim.

### Creation: Build, Don't Only Back
Fund XII reserved ~US$1.5B for "Creation," a strategy that incubates AI-native companies with GC operating partners rather than waiting for external founders — an unusual structure that makes GC part founder, part fund.

### The Platform-generalist Caveat
Unlike AI-only funds, GC spreads across healthcare, defense, fintech and consumer; AI is its fastest-growing mandate but not its whole book. Its value to AI founders is scale capital, government-adjacent networks and the ability to lead US$1B+ rounds few others can.
