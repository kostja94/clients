# Industry Funds（IF）

> 投资人类型档案 · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Investor Types（`/investor-types/industry-fund`）

## 定义

Sector-focused funds backed by governments or industry associations. They aim to build domestic capabilities in strategic technologies.
