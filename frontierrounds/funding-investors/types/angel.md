# Individual Angel Investors（Angel）

> 投资人类型档案 · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Investor Types（`/investor-types/angel`）

## 定义

High-net-worth individuals who invest personal capital at the earliest stages. Many AI angels are former founders, researchers, or operators with deep technical credibility.

| 典型支票 | $25K — $2M |
| AI 相关性 | Critical for pre-product AI labs where technical due diligence matters more than financial metrics. |
