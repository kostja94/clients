# Sovereign Wealth Funds（SWF）

> 投资人类型档案 · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Investor Types（`/investor-types/swf`）

## 定义

State-owned investment funds that manage national savings. They are patient, long-term capital sources that often anchor mega-rounds.
