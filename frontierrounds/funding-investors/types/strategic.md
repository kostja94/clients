# Strategic Investors & Ecosystem Partners（Strategic）

> 投资人类型档案 · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Investor Types（`/investor-types/strategic`）

## 定义

Companies that invest directly in startups to lock in supply, distribution, or product integration. Unlike CVC, these may not be formal venture arms.

## 关键参数

| 项目 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| 典型阶段 | Series A to Series C |
| 典型支票 | $1M — $50M |
| AI 相关性 | Common in AI infrastructure, where compute credits, distribution, and co-development are as valuable as cash. |
