# Private Equity（PE）

> 投资人类型档案 · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Investor Types（`/investor-types/pe`）

## 定义

Funds that acquire or invest in more mature companies, often with a focus on profitability, recurring revenue, and operational improvement.

## 关键参数

| 项目 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| 典型阶段 | Growth to Pre-IPO / Buyout |
| 典型支票 | $50M — $1B+ |
| AI 相关性 | PE is moving into AI-enabled vertical software, data assets, and take-private opportunities. |

## 参考案例

Blackstone (CoreWeave financing) · KKR (Helix Digital Infrastructure) · Thoma Bravo · Vista Equity Partners
