# Accelerators & Incubators（Accel/Inc）

> 投资人类型档案 · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Investor Types（`/investor-types/accelerator`）

## 定义

Programs that provide seed capital, mentorship, and network access in exchange for a small equity stake. Some also run follow-on funds.

## 关键参数

| 项目 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| 典型阶段 | Pre-seed to Seed |
| 典型支票 | $50K — $1M |
| AI 相关性 | Massive feeder system for AI-native startups; many top AI labs trace back to YC and EF cohorts. |
