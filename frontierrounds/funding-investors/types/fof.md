# Fund of Funds（FOF）

> 投资人类型档案 · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Investor Types（`/investor-types/fof`）

## 定义

Investors that allocate capital to other VC, PE, and hedge funds rather than investing directly in startups.

## 关键参数

| 项目 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| 典型阶段 | Indirect (LP in VC funds) |
| 典型支票 | Fund-level commitments |
| AI 相关性 | Indirectly increases AI allocation by backing specialist AI-focused VC managers. |

## 参考案例

Aldea Ventures (AI deep-tech FOF) · AAF Management (Axis Fund) · Draper Fund of Funds
