## 概览

Index Ventures is a transatlantic multi-stage venture and growth firm founded in 1996 in Geneva by Neil Rimer, David Rimer and Giuseppe Zocco, with its operational headquarters in London and additional offices in San Francisco, New York and Jersey. It has backed more than 1,100 companies and raised over US$15B since inception, with roughly US$11.7–13B under management across venture, growth and its Origin seed fund (most recent closings: Index XII US$800M and Growth VII US$1.5B, 2024). Index is the most successful transatlantic venture firm of its generation — Slack, Figma, Revolut, Wiz, Adyen, Datadog, Scale AI — and in the AI cycle it has become the most consistent institutional backer of Europe's flagship lab: it wrote Mistral's first institutional cheque before the company was incorporated and has participated in every round from Seed (2023) through the record €3B Series D (2026). On the US side it holds Scale AI across five rounds, plus Anthropic, Fireworks AI, Cohere and Perplexity.

## 投资逻辑

*How Index Is Playing the AI Cycle*
### People-first, Conviction-driven, Cross-Atlantic
Index describes itself as investing "in who you are, not just what you do," maintaining deep long-term founder relationships rather than transactional allocations. That conviction model is what let Julia Andre write Mistral's first cheque before incorporation and keep following on through the largest European round ever raised.

### Europe's AI Champions as a Core Mandate
Index is the rare top-tier fund with genuine roots on both sides of the Atlantic. Its European franchise — Mistral from seed, Scale AI's European founder Alexandr Wang, plus Cohere and Hippocratic AI in adjacent markets — makes it the default institutional partner for founders building sovereign or enterprise AI from Europe.

### Full-stack AI Exposure
The AI book spans frontier and open-weight labs (Anthropic, Mistral, Cohere), model-serving infrastructure (Fireworks AI, Lightning AI), AI search and applications (Perplexity, Harvey, Hebbia, ArthurAI), and physical AI (Physical Intelligence) — mirroring its multi-stage platform.

### From Seed to the Biggest Rounds
Its fund architecture (Origin seed + venture + Growth VII) lets Index stay in companies from a €105M Mistral seed to a €3B Series D — the same reason it could lead Scale AI's Series B in 2018 and still participate in its 2025 rounds.
