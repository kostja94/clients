## 概览

Eric Schmidt, the former Google CEO, has become one of the most active individual backers of frontier AI through his family office Hillspire and Innovation Endeavors. Hillspire has made 22 investments in private AI companies since 2019. His positions span frontier labs, applied deep tech and defense-adjacent autonomy.

## 详情

Eric Schmidt ran Google from 2001 to 2011 and served as executive chairman until 2019. He co-founded Innovation Endeavors with Dror Berman in 2010. His AI activity is concentrated: in 2022 alone 13 of his 17 startup investments (more than three-quarters) went to AI, per data shared with CNBC by Fintrx.

## 投资逻辑

*How Schmidt Picks AI Companies*

### Frontier Capability and AI Safety as One Bet
His largest known positions are frontier labs — he joined Anthropic's Series A in May 2021 and is a seed shareholder in Mistral — pairing an AI-safety ethos with a bet that capability concentration still wins.

### Own the Hard Science Layer
Through SandboxAQ (quantum + AI, which he chairs), World Labs (spatial AI) and Goodfire (interpretability), Schmidt funds the research-grade layers of the stack rather than thin wrappers.

### Defense and National Security as an AI Category
Unusual among angels, he is a prolific backer of defense technology — Rebellion Defense, White Stork and Istari — informed by his government advisory roles, treating military AI as strategically aligned with frontier capability.

### Deploy Through Two Gears
Hillspire writes family-office checks of any size into later rounds; Innovation Endeavors does classic early-stage venture. Both report to the same thesis, which is why Schmidt can appear at a Mistral seed and an Anthropic growth round.
