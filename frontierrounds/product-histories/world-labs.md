## 叙事章节

*The Story Behind the Numbers*

### The Godmother of AI and Spatial Intelligence
Fei-Fei Li built ImageNet, the benchmark that helped seed modern deep learning, then led AI/ML at Google Cloud and co-directed Stanford's Institute for Human-Centered AI. She frames "spatial intelligence" — the ability to perceive, reason about and act within 3D space — as AI's next frontier, as fundamental to intelligence as language. Her November 2025 manifesto, *From Words to Worlds*, argued that today's LLMs lack understanding of the physically, geometrically and dynamically complex world, and that world models are "the defining challenge of the next decade."

### From Research Lab to Product Company
World Labs' first product, Marble, shipped on 12 November 2025 after a September beta, letting anyone generate persistent, high-fidelity 3D worlds from text, images, video or coarse 3D layouts — exportable for tools like Unreal and Unity across freemium tiers. A programmable World API followed on 21 January 2026, signalling the shift from demo to developer platform. In July 2026 World Labs acquired SceniX and published early real-to-sim-to-real results — policies trained entirely in simulation transferred across robot platforms and ran for hours on hardware. On 1 September 2026 it unveiled Atlas, an "omni" world model pretrained from scratch for generation, reconstruction and simulation, entering early access with select partners.

### The World-model Race
World Labs competes with Google DeepMind's Genie, NVIDIA's Cosmos platform and startups such as Decart and Odyssey — with Marble differentiated by generating persistent, downloadable 3D environments rather than on-the-fly video. Two of its largest rivals' backers, NVIDIA and AMD, are also World Labs investors, reflecting the sector's dual partner-and-competitor dynamic. Yann LeCun's AMI Labs raised concurrently at a ~US$3.5B valuation, underscoring surging appetite for physical/spatial AI. The valuation logic from ~US$1B to a reported ~US$5B in ~17 months rests on syndicate confidence and product momentum — Marble's GA, World API adoption and the robotics push — rather than disclosed revenue.

### A Shifting Investor Base
The 2024 seed assembled a signature academic-Silicon-Valley syndicate: VC co-leads a16z, NEA and Radical Ventures, corporate VCs (NVentures, AMD, Intel, Adobe, Databricks) and a who's-who of individual angels. The February 2026 round marked a structural shift toward strategic and institutional capital with direct commercial stakes in 3D and physical AI: Autodesk anchored with US$200M and an advisory seat, NVIDIA and AMD doubled down, and Fidelity brought large asset-manager money. The arc — from star-founder angel enthusiasm to mission-critical industry co-investment — tracks World Labs' own evolution from research lab to commercial platform vendor with a robotics roadmap.
