## 叙事章节

*The Story Behind the Numbers*

### From Snap's GenAI Chief to the Attention Economy

Alex Mashrabov co-founded AI Factory — the personalized-video startup Snap acquired for US$166M in 2020 — then ran Snap's generative-AI work as Head of Generative AI, shipping the MyAI chatbot (150M+ users) and viral GenAI AR effects. In September 2023 he left to found Higgsfield with Kazakh AI researcher Yerzat Dulat (CTO) and veteran operator Mahi de Silva, spending about a year in stealth before launching a browser-based "click-to-video" platform in March 2025. The thesis: build for the attention economy, where social-media marketers and brands need cinematic, character-consistent video at scale, not just prompt-based clips.

### The Fastest Ramp in AI Video

Higgsfield's trajectory compressed several years of growth into months. The platform went from US$0 to US$10M ARR within weeks of its March 2025 launch, to ~US$100M within five months, to US$200M annual run rate by January 2026 (doubling in about two months), and to US$700M annualized by the August 2026 Series B — a pace the company benchmarked against Lovable, Cursor, OpenAI, Slack and Zoom. Users grew from 11M (September 2025) to 15M+ (January 2026) to 30M+ across 200 countries, with professional social-media marketers driving roughly 85% of activity.

### A Valuation That Quadrupled in Eight Months

The Series B's US$5.4B price more than quadrupled the US$1.3B set in January 2026, itself up from a ~US$1B unicorn round five months earlier. The round's shape is the subplot: DST Global led, while a strategic tier of corporate and sovereign investors — Intel Capital, Goldman Sachs Alternatives' Growth Equity, Liberty Global Tech Ventures, Mirae Asset Capital and NTT DOCOMO Ventures — signalled that compute, connectivity and distribution giants see Higgsfield as infrastructure for the next era of visual media. CEO Mashrabov framed compute as the moat: one minute of video is like processing 60,000 words, making reserved capacity a competitive necessity against Synthesia and Runway.

### Kazakhstan's First Unicorn Goes Global

Higgsfield was widely reported as Kazakhstan's first unicorn at the September 2025 Series A — a point of national and diaspora significance given Mashrabov and Dulat's origins. The Series B doubled down on the APAC path: Korea's Mirae Asset Capital and Japan's NTT DOCOMO Ventures joined as strategic investors, with proceeds earmarked for a dedicated Asia-Pacific go-to-market team, alongside the Higgsfield Academy and the Higgsfield For Good program (partnering with NGO YGA to put AI visual tools in the hands of 70,000 students and 13,000 educators).

### From Viral Toy to Enterprise Backbone

Higgsfield's early notoriety came from celebrity viral videos — Madonna, Will Smith and Snoop Dogg were named users — but the company has worked to reposition as enterprise infrastructure, with Marketing Studio for advertisers and Cinema Studio for filmmakers, and agentic products (after the May 2026 Supercomputer rollout) that automate multi-scene production and grew 42x in three months. The durable question is the same one facing every consumer-born AI tool: whether it can consolidate into a platform with staying power before the next hot model arrives.
