## 叙事章节

*The Story Behind the Numbers*

### From Roommate Demo to Legal AI's Biggest Company
Pereyra showed Weinberg OpenAI's GPT-3 and Weinberg — a former O'Melveny & Myers securities litigator — realized it could transform legal workflows. The two launched Harvey in 2022, and the startup became the category's defining vendor: by September 2026 it counted ~80% of top US law firms as customers, and its valuation has risen from US$11B (March) to US$15.6B (September), a 42% jump in six months with revenue roughly doubling over the same period.

### Own the Model, Own the Category
Harvey's 2026 strategy shift is toward its own models: weeks before the September round it launched Harvey Tenet, its first post-trained open-weight legal model (based on Moonshot's Kimi K3), trained specifically for legal tasks. The US$550M round is earmarked for model talent and compute — moving beyond building on top of OpenAI/Anthropic models.

### Four Acquisitions and a Platform Play
The Guardrails AI deal (agent-security testing, co-founders Shreya Rajpal and Zayd Simjee joining Harvey's product org) is Harvey's fourth acquisition of 2026. Founder Weinberg says more are likely, treating deals largely as acqui-hires for strong teams — a consolidation pattern that matches Lightspeed's view that legal is the second-largest addressable AI market after code.

### A Valuation Justified by Market Size
Lightspeed estimates legal is the second-largest addressable AI market, and that market argument underpins the ~US$15.6B valuation. With >US$1.5B raised, Harvey is simultaneously the most valuable and best-funded company in legal AI — the reference point against which every legal-AI competitor (Legora, Robin AI, Spellbook, etc.) is now measured.
