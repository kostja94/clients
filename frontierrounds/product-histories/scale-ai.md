## 叙事章节

*The Story Behind the Numbers*

### The Picks-and-shovels Company of the AI Era
Scale turned data labeling — the unglamorous work of teaching models what the world looks like — into a mission-critical business for every frontier lab, from autonomous vehicles to foundation models. Its customers included OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Meta, making it the shared data layer beneath the competing model stacks.

### From YC Seed to the AI Boom's Biggest Strategic Check
A US$120K YC seed in 2016 became a US$13.8B Series F in 2024 and a ~US$29B valuation by 2025. The valuation ladder — US$1B (2019), US$3.5B (2020), US$7.3B (2021), US$13.8B (2024), ~US$29B (2025) — tracks the AI boom almost exactly.

### The Meta Deal That Rewired the Business
Meta's June 2025 investment of ~US$14.3B for ~49% non-voting control was a strategic masterstroke and a commercial earthquake: it removed the conflict of one AI giant owning a stake in the shared data layer, and it sent Scale's other hyperscaler customers to competitors. Wang's departure to Meta — while retaining his board seat — blurred the line between Scale and its largest investor.

### Independence in Name
Scale insists it remains independent — Meta holds no voting power, and Droege's interim CEO appointment kept an operating executive in charge. But with its founder at Meta, ~49% of the company owned by Meta, and its former customers building in-house alternatives, the ~US$29B valuation is a bet on Scale's continued neutrality that the 2025 churn put in question.
