## 叙事章节

*The Story Behind the Numbers*

### The AlphaGo Architect Leaves DeepMind
David Silver led the DeepMind team behind AlphaGo — the first program to beat a world champion at Go — then AlphaZero (self-play mastery of chess, shogi and Go), AlphaStar, MuZero and AlphaProof. He spent over a decade as head of DeepMind's reinforcement learning group, holds a UCL professorship and ~300,000 citations. Fortune first reported his departure on 30 January 2026; the company entity had been registered the previous November, and Silver's personal note dated 15 January 2026 explained his decision to build a lab aimed at "making first contact with superintelligence."

### The Era of Experience
Ineffable's core wager is that LLMs — trained to imitate human text — will plateau before superintelligence. With Richard Sutton, Silver wrote *Welcome to the Era of Experience* (2025), arguing the next leap comes when an agent's own experience, not human data, becomes the dominant learning signal. The company is building a "superlearner": no imitation, just continuous learning from the consequences of its own actions inside simulated worlds, rediscovering and then transcending human inventions such as language and mathematics. Critics note RL has so far triumphed mainly where rules and reward signals are explicit — nobody has shown it scaling to open-ended tasks with ambiguous rewards.

### Europe's Sovereign-AI Flagship Bet
The seed — co-led by US funds Sequoia and Lightspeed, with NVIDIA and Google participating — made Ineffable the UK's flagship sovereign-AI bet: the newly launched Sovereign AI Fund (£500M, opened 16 April 2026) took its second direct equity position alongside a US$20M British Business Bank investment, with the science secretary framing it as ensuring "the UK isn't just an AI taker but an AI maker." Within Europe it contrasts with France's open-weight champion Mistral; its closest ideological neighbours are the emerging RL labs — Sutton left to found his own lab in July 2026, and Recursive Superintelligence raised US$650M in May 2026.

### Team, Compute, and What Comes Next
On 7 September 2026 the company elevated six senior hires to co-founder: four ex-DeepMind researchers (Chris Apps, Wojciech Czarnecki, Lasse Espeholt, Junhyuk Oh), ex-InstaDeep research head Alexandre Laterre, and former Flying Fish partner Heather Gorham. Progress so far is infrastructure: an NVIDIA engineering collaboration to co-design large-scale RL infrastructure (May 2026) and Google Cloud as compute partner running one of the largest A5X clusters (June 2026). There is still no public product, model, paper or revenue — five months after the largest seed round Europe has ever seen.
