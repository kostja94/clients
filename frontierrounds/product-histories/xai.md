## 叙事章节

*The Story Behind the Numbers*

### Colossus as the Moat
xAI's defining asset is not Grok but Colossus — a Memphis training cluster built at record speed to 1M+ H100-equivalent GPUs. The capital raises of 2024–2026 map directly to this hardware buildout: each round bought more GPUs, and the July 2025 US$10B package plus an off-balance-sheet US$20B GPU-leasing SPV (Nvidia + asset managers, October 2025) extended the model without diluting equity.

### Merging the Lab With the Platform
The March 2025 all-stock acquisition of X (xAI at US$80B) gave Grok exclusive access to X's real-time data and distribution. The SpaceX merger eleven months later (US$250B) folded the lab into Musk's broader aerospace-and-compute empire, ending xAI's life as an independent company.

### Valuation Velocity and Contested Rounds
From US$5B (December 2023) to US$250B (February 2026) is roughly a 50x expansion in 26 months — the fastest valuation arc in the frontier-lab set. It came with unusual noise: Musk publicly called the September 2025 Series D report "fake news" before its close was corroborated, and Tesla's US$2B Series E commitment remained subject to shareholder approval.

### From Lab to Subsidiary
The February 2026 SpaceX acquisition at US$250B was effectively an exit for xAI's outside investors, who swapped into SpaceX equity. Post-SpaceX-IPO (June 2026), the combined entity's ~US$1.75T valuation made the xAI round history a prologue to the Musk capital machine rather than a standalone story.
