# OpenAI

> The lab that started the modern AI boom, now the most heavily funded private company in history

> 产品融资时间线档案 · 类型：product · 数据来自 frontierrounds.com Product Histories（`/products/openai`）

## 概览

OpenAI is the San Francisco research-and-deployment lab behind ChatGPT, GPT and Sora, founded in December 2015 as a nonprofit and restructured around a capped-profit subsidiary in 2019. Its funding arc defines the AI era: a US$1B Microsoft partnership in 2019, a US$10B Microsoft commitment at a ~US$29B valuation in January 2023, a US$6.6B round at US$157B in October 2024, a US$40B round at US$300B in March 2025 (then the largest private tech deal ever), and a record US$122B close at an US$852B post-money valuation in March 2026 — anchored by Amazon (up to US$50B), Nvidia (US$30B) and SoftBank (US$30B). OpenAI generates about US$2B in monthly revenue (2026), serves 900M+ weekly ChatGPT users, and is preparing a public listing. Cumulative funding is roughly US$142B on Frontier Rounds' conservative disclosed basis; third-party trackers that include credit facilities and rumored rounds put the figure at US$180–197B.

## 叙事章节

*The Story Behind the Numbers*

### From Nonprofit Mission to the Most Valuable Startup Ever
OpenAI began in 2015 as a nonprofit pledged US$1B to develop AI "for the benefit of humanity," restructured around a capped-profit subsidiary in 2019 to fund compute, then reincorporated as a public-benefit corporation in October 2025 as it prepared for public markets. Each structural step unlocked a step-change in capital — the 2019 Microsoft deal, the 2023 US$10B, the US$157B→US$300B→US$852B valuation ladder of 2024–2026.

### Product-led Revenue Engine
ChatGPT turned OpenAI from a research lab into the fastest-growing consumer application in history. By March 2026 ChatGPT passed 900M weekly active users and 50M+ subscribers, with monthly revenue crossing US$2B; Microsoft integrates OpenAI models across Copilot and Windows, and OpenAI's enterprise base passed five million business users.

### A Three-hyperscaler Cap Table
OpenAI's register is unique in spanning Microsoft (compute partner since 2019), SoftBank (co-lead of the 2025 and 2026 rounds) and Amazon (up to US$50B in 2026) simultaneously — the first time the three cloud giants share one frontier lab's cap table, with Nvidia as strategic investor and GPU supplier.

### The Trillion-dollar Compute Race
OpenAI's capital is spent on a ~US$600B cumulative compute program through 2030 (reported target), including Stargate data centres; the US$4.7B revolving credit facility closed alongside the 2026 round was undrawn at close. A US$2B round in early 2025 and later tranches have funded frontier training runs that each cost more than entire previous funding generations.

### IPO Ahead
With confidential filing expected in 2026, OpenAI's US$852B private valuation is the staging ground for what would be one of the largest public listings ever. Whether the round's conditional components (Amazon's US$35B tied to IPO/AGI milestones) convert depends on hitting both a public debut and AGI progress.
